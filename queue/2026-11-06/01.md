Pedro Neto rejected Manchester City to sign an upgraded multi-year deal at Stamford Bridge.

⚽ Chelsea's hierarchy has established a policy of offering long-term, incentive-heavy contracts while remaining flexible enough to reward top performers with pay raises. Since joining from Wolves for £54m in 2024, Neto has collected 40 goal involvements and adapted smoothly to Xabi Alonso's system as an attacking wingback.

💼 The Portuguese winger's commitment directly contrasts with Enzo Fernández, who pushed for a move, requested a transfer, and was subsequently sold to Manchester City for £125m. Chelsea viewed Fernández's conduct as disruptive, making Neto's dedication to the project a clear benchmark for how the club intends to reward loyalty.

📊 Neto joins Cole Palmer, Moisés Caicedo, and João Pedro in securing improved terms as Chelsea double down on keeping core players happy.
