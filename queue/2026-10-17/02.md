Arsenal have secured their biggest sale ever as Gabriel Martinelli joins Al-Hilal for £60m.

🇸🇦 Martinelli’s move to Al-Hilal marks a historic exit for Arsenal. The £60m transfer eclipses the £40m fee Liverpool paid for Alex Oxlade-Chamberlain in 2017, setting a brand-new benchmark for player sales at the North London club.

💰 The Brazilian forward, who originally arrived from Ituano in 2019, has signed a four-year deal in Saudi Arabia reported to be worth £17m per season. Arsenal have also protected their future upside by inserting a sell-on clause into the agreement.

🔄 Having fallen down the Emirates pecking order, Martinelli becomes part of a broader late squad reshape that saw Gabriel Jesus move to Barcelona and Fábio Vieira join Hamburg.
