🚀 Just three years before Chelsea broke their club record to sign him for £117m, Morgan Rogers was struggling to get games in the Championship. His value had plummeted to just £1m during difficult loan spells away from Manchester City.

📉 At Bournemouth, the forward started only three matches under Scott Parker. Former scout Des Taylor revealed Rogers was signed late in the window to play as a number ten, but the manager's system didn't use one, leaving him isolated and out of position.

💪 Rather than letting the setback derail his career, Rogers credits these difficult loans with transforming him from an academy prospect into a resilient professional. Bournemouth passed on a £9m option, a decision they might now regret.

Would Rogers have reached this level without those early career struggles?
