Could Manchester City's owners actually sell the club? Guilty verdicts have sparked fears of a shock exit. 

🚨 Manchester City's future has been thrown into complete uncertainty. An independent commission has found the club guilty of the majority of their financial charges. The findings allege that City used sham contracts with commercial partners to disguise secret funding worth over £830m.

💼 The fallout is drawing political concern, with Prime Minister Andy Burnham expressing deep worry over losing the owners. Since 2008, Sheikh Mansour's group has poured massive investment into both the club and the city of Manchester. A potential exit would halt one of the most powerful projects in modern football.

⚖️ What happens next? The club has until October 2 to appeal, but the newly established Independent Football Regulator is already looking into stripping the owners of their licence. Sanctions could range from heavy point deductions and massive fines to outright expulsion from the Premier League.
