🚨 Manchester City’s golden era faces a historic crisis after a commission exposed the mechanics behind their £1.2bn empire.\n\nAccording to the commission’s findings, City’s owners bypassed financial fair play rules by injecting £830 million of direct funding masked as external sponsorship deals between 2009 and 2018. When factoring in falsified image rights and player contracts, that figure climbs to a massive £920 million.\n\nWithout this artificially inflated revenue, the club simply could not have complied with financial rules while spending £1.2 billion on transfers. The findings suggest iconic signings like Yaya Toure, David Silva, and Sergio Aguero may never have arrived at the Etihad under legal spending limits.\n\nWhile the titles won on the pitch cannot be replayed, rival clubs are already exploring legal avenues for financial compensation. The era that defined modern City now carries a permanent regulatory asterisk.\n\nShould City be stripped of their historic titles?
