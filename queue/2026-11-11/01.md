Arsenal have smashed their club sale record after sanctioning Gabriel Martinelli's £60m move to Al-Hilal.

💸 The £60m package eclipses the £40m Arsenal received for Alex Oxlade-Chamberlain in 2017, making Martinelli the most expensive departure in Gunners history. Arsenal have also secured a sell-on clause in the transaction.

🇸🇦 For Martinelli, the transfer lands him a four-year contract in Saudi Arabia reportedly worth around £17m per season. Having seen reduced game time since last season's league triumph, the 25-year-old completes his move before the Saudi transfer deadline.

📋 The Gunners continue a major squad clearout after sanctioning exits for other fringe names without bringing in late additions.
