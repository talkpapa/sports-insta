Manchester City have agreed a €100m deal for Lille's teenage midfielder Ayyoub Bouaddi, funded by a major departure.

💰 The English champions are paying €95m (£81.4m) upfront plus €5m in potential add-ons for the 18-year-old Moroccan international. This transfer takes their summer spending to nearly £200m.

🔄 This massive outlay is heavily supported by Rodri's £65m sale to Barcelona last week. Bouaddi, who became the youngest player in Conference League history at age 16, will complete his medical in the coming days.

📋 Capable of playing as a defensive midfielder, Bouaddi is not expected to start immediately. City already secured Elliot Anderson for £116m and are keeping tabs on Chelsea's Enzo Fernández to further bolster their options.
