🚨 Folarin Balogun's £40m transfer to Everton fell through at the final hour because the striker felt disrespected by how Everton handled his medical tests.💼 According to Monaco chief executive Thiago Scuro, Everton raised sudden concerns about Balogun's health around 7pm on deadline day and tried renegotiating the payment structure. While Everton claimed Monaco delayed sending medical files, Monaco insisted there were no health issues. The medical concerns quickly leaked to the press, frustrating the 25-year-old American international.🔄 The fallout created a domino effect across Europe. Monaco had initially agreed to sell Lamine Camara to Chelsea for £47m as a backup plan, but pulled out once Everton agreed to proceed with Balogun.
