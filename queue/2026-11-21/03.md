NFL legend Tom Brady could soon acquire an ownership stake in Chelsea through private equity firm Clearlake Capital.⚽ According to reports, former NFL quarterback Tom Brady is targeting a non-voting equity position in Chelsea by investing directly through main shareholder Clearlake Capital. Brady recently ended his involvement with Birmingham City and maintains strong ties with Clearlake co-founder Behdad Eghbali.💰 Crucially, sources clarify that this transaction is completely separate from Todd Boehly and Mark Walter's ongoing £950m equity buyout by Clearlake. Brady's entry would not dilute existing control or provide him with decision-making power, though a ceremonial board seat remains possible.📈 For Clearlake, adding a high-profile US sports icon boosts Chelsea's commercial potential across North American markets without surrendering operational command over transfer strategy or club governance.Would you like to see celebrity sports figures taking non-voting stakes in Premier League clubs?
