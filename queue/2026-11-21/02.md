Manchester United's managerial merry-go-round is about to cross the £100m threshold following their latest appointment.

💸 Stock market filings reveal United borrowed £10m three days after announcing Mauricio Pochettino as head coach. A £7.5m compensation fee is owed to US Soccer to release the manager, pushing total managerial payout costs since 2013 past £95m.

📊 Financial pressures continue to mount with £417m in deferred transfer fees still unpaid, including £181m due within twelve months. The decision to dismiss Michael Carrick, who signed a two-year contract in the summer, further inflates severance costs and pushes overall debt to £577.4m.

⚽ Pochettino becomes the ninth full-time boss since Sir Alex Ferguson retired. With three mid-season managerial changes in consecutive campaigns, squad overhaul costs and severance liabilities are stretching financial flexibility to its limit.

Can Pochettino finally break the costly cycle at Old Trafford?
