Manchester United chief Omar Berrada has addressed the decision to replace Michael Carrick with Mauricio Pochettino. 🚨 A disastrous start to the campaign left United 18th in the Premier League table, triggering Carrick's dismissal during the November international break. Mauricio Pochettino now takes charge immediately ahead of a high-stakes debut against rivals Liverpool. 💬 Pitch struggles coincided with unprecedented internal discord. Squad members publicly targeted leadership, with Lisandro Martinez questioning technical director Jason Wilcox over transfer strategy and Bruno Fernandes raising concerns over tactical preparation. 📊 Speaking on the turmoil, CEO Omar Berrada acknowledged failing to achieve stability after appointing three permanent managers in under three years. Despite admitting mistakes in squad planning and leadership scrutiny, Berrada insisted structural foundations are being established for sustained success.
