Ronaldinho is back on the pitch at 46, starting for Serie C club Ravenna 11 years after stepping away from professional football.

🇧🇷 The 2005 Ballon d'Or winner made a surprise 45-minute appearance for Italian third-tier side Ravenna against local rivals Forlì. The club even built a temporary 1,800-seat stand named 'R10' to accommodate the packed crowd at the Stadio Bruno Benelli.

💼 Beyond the headline stunt, Ronaldinho has actually taken an ownership stake as a shareholder in Ravenna. He agreed to play after personal requests from the club president, helping build massive global interest and publicity for the Serie C outfit.

⚽ While he did not score before being substituted at half-time, he played a key role in Ravenna's opening goal and hinted he could return for more games if the president calls on him again.
