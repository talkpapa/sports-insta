Brighton may have secured the bargain of deadline day by bringing in Millwall winger Azeez for £12.5m.

📊 Brighton's recruitment department has built a reputation on identifying statistical anomalies in lower leagues. In Azeez, they found a winger who generated 9.12 expected assists in the Championship last season but saw teammates convert only seven of those chances into goals.

💰 According to reports, the deal is structured at an initial £12.5m with potential add-ons taking the final package to £15m. The wide midfielder also ranked fourth in the second tier for dribble attempts and created 18 big chances, confirming his status as an elite ball-carrier and crossing specialist.

🎯 By placing his high-volume crossing into a squad with higher-caliber finishers, Brighton expect his assist numbers to rise dramatically in the Premier League.
