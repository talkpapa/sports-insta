Folarin Balogun aborted a £40m move to Everton at the final hour because he felt insulted by how they handled his medical.

📝 Deadline day drama reached a peak when Everton raised medical concerns around 7 PM and leaked the doubts to the press. Monaco insisted their tests showed no issues, but Everton spent hours consulting outside doctors while attempting to renegotiate terms.

❌ By 10 PM, Everton finally cleared Balogun and signed the deal sheet to grant a deadline extension. However, Balogun refused to sign his contract, stating he was uncomfortable joining a club that publicly questioned his health.

🔄 The collapse created a massive domino effect in the market. Monaco had lined up a £47m sale of Lamine Camara to Chelsea as a backup, but pulled out of that deal once Everton re-entered for Balogun, leaving Chelsea empty-handed.
