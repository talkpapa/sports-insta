Bradley Barcola has completed a £123m move to Liverpool after falling down the pecking order at Paris Saint-Germain.

🇫🇷 The French international spent three seasons in Paris after joining from Lyon for €45m, but the arrival of Khvicha Kvaratskhelia in January 2025 severely limited his starting opportunities in big matches.

💰 PSG accepted Liverpool's £123m offer to pocket a profit of nearly €100m, marking the largest departure fee in Ligue 1 history and contributing to a record summer of sales for the French champions.

🔴 Having supported Liverpool since his academy days at Lyon, Barcola made an Anfield transfer his top priority to secure guaranteed minutes on his preferred left flank.
