Mikel Arteta has signed a contract extension until 2030 worth over £20m a season, securing total authority at Arsenal.

📝 According to reports, the new four-year deal doubles his previous salary and places him alongside the highest-paid managers in world football. Arteta’s direct relationship with co-chair Josh Kroenke has solidified his position as the central figure across all major club operations.

🎯 His influence extends well beyond matchdays, with sources indicating he holds final decision-making power on transfer strategy, squad structure, and key stadium infrastructure choices. This includes vetoing late window moves to preserve pathways for top academy talent like Max Dowman.

⚽ By committing long term, Arsenal have established a unified hierarchy where managerial strategy drives every executive decision.
