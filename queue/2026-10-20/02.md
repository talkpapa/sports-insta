Former Premier League forward Saido Berahino has retired at 33 to take up a coaching role with Burundi.

⚽ Once one of English football's brightest young talents, Berahino scored 20 goals in all competitions for West Brom in 2014-15 before securing a £12m transfer to Stoke City in January 2017.

💼 His move to Stoke struggled to reignite his form, leading to an early contract termination by mutual consent in 2019 and a nomadic journey through Belgium, Cyprus, India, and Slovenia.

🇹🇩 Now, the Burundi international hangs up his boots to transition into full-time coaching for his home nation.

Can Berahino build a successful second career on the touchline?
