🚨 Matthias Jaissle is feeling the heat at Newcastle United just three months into a four-year contract. 📉 The Magpies are enduring their worst home stretch in modern history, suffering seven home defeats in 2026 and failing to keep a clean sheet in 14 straight home league matches. Fans at St James' Park voiced their anger with loud boos after a second defeat to Everton in a single week. 💸 Behind the collapse is a massive summer transition. The club departed with former manager Eddie Howe and sanctioned major outgoings, selling vital starters Anthony Gordon, Sandro Tonali, and Bruno Guimaraes. Replacing experienced leaders with raw talent left the squad severely stripped of core composure. ⚙️ Jaissle's attempts to implement a high-energy Red Bull pressing style have stumbled without the tactical foundation, leaving Newcastle lacking confidence when trailing. With tough fixtures against Fulham, Arsenal, and Brighton ahead, the pressure on the board's long-term vision is mounting fast. Can Jaissle turn Newcastle's project around before the board loses patience?
