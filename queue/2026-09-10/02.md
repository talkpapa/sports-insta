Lionel Messi is expanding his football empire with a preliminary agreement to buy Spanish second-division side Eldense.\n\n💼 Reports from Spain confirm that the Inter Miami forward is set to become the majority shareholder of Eldense. The club announced a preliminary agreement has been reached to acquire the shares currently held by Grupo TH Soluciones, marking Messi's second major club investment in Spain.\n\n⚖️ The takeover is not yet fully complete. The transaction must undergo a formal due diligence process and requires final official authorization from the Spanish National Sports Council before the ownership transition is officially sealed.\n\n📈 This move expands Messi's growing off-pitch business portfolio. He already owns fifth-tier Catalan side Cornella, a purchase aimed at strengthening his historical ties to Spanish football and helping develop local sporting talent in the region.\n\nWill Messi's ownership help save struggling Eldense from relegation?
