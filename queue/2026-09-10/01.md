📈 Why are Premier League giants sending their best teenagers to battle in League One and Two? Here is the strategy.

⚽ Academy football is highly technical, but it often lacks the sheer physical intensity of senior professional leagues. Top clubs are utilizing EFL loans to harden prospects like Spurs' forward Melia and City's goalkeeper Whatmuff. Experiencing weekly competitive pressure is vital for their development.

🛡️ Facing experienced EFL defenders teaches young players how to survive under pressure. Going up against physical, senior opponents forces rapid tactical maturity, helping raw talents bridge the massive gap between youth academies and first-team Premier League action.
