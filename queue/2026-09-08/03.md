🇺🇸 Shrewsbury Town are finally set for American ownership after the EFL and regulators cleared a long-awaited takeover bid.

After years of frustration, including multiple collapsed takeover attempts since 2024, Shrewsbury Town fans finally have clarity. Regulators have officially approved an as-yet-unnamed American consortium to acquire the club, bringing an end to Roland Wycherley's historic 30-year tenure as owner.

The takeover comes at a critical sporting juncture. After relegation from League One, the Shrews barely escaped dropping out of the Football League entirely last term. A lack of investment saw them miss out on primary summer transfer targets, leaving the squad desperately short of quality.

While the final documentation is being drawn up, the immediate priority for the new owners will be stabilizing the club's recruitment and backing manager Gavin Cowan. This financial injection is the only way to modernize a club that has stalled since its stadium move.
