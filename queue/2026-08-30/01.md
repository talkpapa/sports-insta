🚨 West Ham broke the Championship transfer record this summer, but a winless start has put Nuno under massive pressure.

Relegated from the Premier League, West Ham were expected to breeze through the second tier. Management backed this ambition by splashing a division-record £22m on midfielder Arne Engels, explicitly targeting an instant top-flight return.

Instead, the heavy investment has created an unforgiving environment. After a draw against Watford—where they managed just one shot on target and relied on an own goal to equalise—travelling fans targeted manager Nuno Espírito Santo with sack chants.

With only two points from three matches, Nuno insists his expensive new squad just needs time to click. But in the Championship, record-breaking transfer statements leave very little room for early-season slip-ups.

Can Nuno turn this around, or did the record fee create impossible expectations?
