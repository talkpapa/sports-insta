Danny Ings has officially signed for League One side Wycombe Wanderers as a free agent. ⚽ The 34-year-old former England striker was unattached following his spell at Sheffield United. Sitting 14th in the table and winless at home under caretaker charge, Wycombe desperately required proven goalscoring output without committing to a transfer fee. 💼 Existing relationships sped up negotiations, as Wycombe coach Sam Vokes previously played alongside Ings at Burnley. That personal connection helped convince the veteran forward to drop to the third tier for guaranteed game time. 📊 Having completed full training sessions, Ings is already match-fit and eligible to make his debut against Reading this weekend. Can Danny Ings turn Wycombe's season around?
