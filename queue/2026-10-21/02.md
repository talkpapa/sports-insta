Benching an £11m record signing is a bold move, but Celtic's 7-1 win over Hearts proved why manager Martin O'Neill did it.

⚽ Following a difficult Europa League loss against Benfica where Kasper Hogh was substituted at half-time, Celtic rearranged their frontline. With Hogh scoring just once in 12 appearances, O'Neill shifted Camilo Duran into a central role alongside Callum Osmand and Haissem Hassan.

📊 The results were immediate. Duran brought superior mobility and pressing to the central striker position, taking his tally to 9 goals and 5 assists in 15 matches. Although Hogh remains a massive financial investment, Celtic's front three currently looks far more dangerous without him in the starting XI.
