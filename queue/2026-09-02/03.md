⚡ Mykhailo Mudryk is heading to North London in one of the most intriguing deadline-day moves.

Tottenham have secured the Chelsea winger on loan until the end of the season, negotiated with a steep £75 million option to make the transfer permanent. It represents a massive gamble for Spurs, who have already spent over £300 million this summer and also added Chelsea defender Tosin Adarabioyo to their ranks.

The driving force behind this deal is Roberto De Zerbi. Having worked with Mudryk at Shakhtar Donetsk, the Spurs manager convinced the hierarchy to pivot to him after failing to land Cody Gakpo. Mudryk has not played competitively since November 2024 following a provisional FA suspension for a banned substance, which was officially resolved this July.

Can De Zerbi finally unlock Mudryk's true potential?
