🏟️ Why is one of the world's most modern stadiums still named simply 'Tottenham Hotspur Stadium'? Despite opening in 2019 and hosting massive non-football events, Spurs are yet to secure a lucrative naming rights deal.\n\nThe club is holding out for an elite, renowned brand that aligns perfectly with their values. Because the venue hosts up to 30 major non-football events a year, including NFL games and concerts, Spurs believe the naming rights are worth a premium and refuse to undervalue the asset in a competitive market.\n\nTo generate revenue in the meantime, Tottenham launched 'The Collective,' a program allowing partners like Sports Illustrated to sponsor individual stands. This helps them raise commercial income while they wait for the perfect stadium partner.\n\nShould Spurs lower their price to secure a sponsor now?
