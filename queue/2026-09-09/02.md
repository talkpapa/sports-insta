🇦🇷 Alexis Mac Allister has revealed his deep disappointment after Liverpool declined to offer him a contract extension this summer.

😔 Despite interest from major clubs like Real Madrid and Manchester City, the 27-year-old midfielder chose to stay at Anfield. However, he was left unhappy when the club informed him that no contract extension would be offered, even as teammates Dominik Szoboszlai and Ryan Gravenberch signed new terms.

📉 The decision comes down to leverage. Mac Allister arrived for a cut-price £35m via a release clause, which secured him a highly lucrative wage from day one. With two years remaining on his current deal and coming off a difficult season, Liverpool's hierarchy is choosing to wait before committing to an older player.
