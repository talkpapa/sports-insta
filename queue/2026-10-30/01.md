Inter and AC Milan are leaving San Siro behind for a €1.2B multi-use arena.

🏗️ Both Milan clubs have officially presented plans for Generali Stadium, a 71,500-capacity venue scheduled to open for the 2031-32 season. Construction on the site of the current ground is slated to begin in 2028 after both sides jointly paid €197 million for the existing site.

💰 San Siro has stood since 1926, but its age severely limits matchday income. The new design by Foster + Partners features steep stands brought right to the pitch edge, alongside full infrastructure to host NFL international games, rugby, and major entertainment events.

🇮🇹 Beyond year-round commercial revenue, the facility gives Italy a showcase venue for Euro 2032. Despite fan opposition over demolishing an iconic ground, structural limits and financial demands pushed both boards to act.
