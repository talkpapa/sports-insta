🧱 Leeds United's £34.1m move for Tarik Muharemovic was questioned in July, but he has quickly silenced the doubters.

📌 After selling long-serving defender Pascal Struijk to Brighton for £20m, Leeds needed a tall, left-footed centre-back to step straight into their starting lineup. They invested £34.1m to bring in 23-year-old Muharemovic from Sassuolo on a five-year contract.

📊 The Bosnian international has repaid that faith immediately, transforming Leeds into the Premier League's second-meanest defence behind Arsenal. His outstanding form and dominant aerial presence have reportedly caught the eye of Barcelona, Liverpool, Bayern Munich, and Inter Milan.

Can Leeds hold onto Muharemovic past his debut season if giant European clubs make a formal move?
