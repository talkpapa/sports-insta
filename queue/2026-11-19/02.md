MLS summer spending just hit nearly $190m—a massive 65% jump driven by roster rule updates and record player exports.

💸 The surge comes as the league fully embraces traditional cash-for-player transfers, moving away from sole reliance on internal allocation currency. Teams are now permitted to convert up to $3m in eligible transfer income into allocation funds annually, giving front offices unprecedented financial flexibility.

📈 Outbound transfers are fuelling this growth from the bottom up. MLS teams have banked almost $800m in player sales since 2022, with European clubs paying top fees for young talent such as Zavier Gozo and Lucas Herrington.

🔄 As clubs rebuild front office operations to exploit these updated regulations, total league expenditure surpassed $410m across the full year. This dual engine of academy monetization and updated roster mechanics is reshaping how the league operates globally.
