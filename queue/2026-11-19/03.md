Streaming platforms are ready to drop $2 billion to capture the 2030 World Cup US broadcast rights.

📺 FIFA has opened the bidding process for the 2030 World Cup in Spain, Portugal, and Morocco, expecting offers between $1.5 billion and $2 billion. For the first time ever, FIFA is selling both English and Spanish-language US rights together, creating a winner-take-all package designed specifically for deep-pocketed global platforms.

💰 Traditional networks like Fox face fierce competition from Netflix, Apple TV, Amazon, and YouTube. Netflix is considered a primary contender after already securing US rights for the 2027 and 2031 Women's World Cups. Network executives expect ad revenues to soar following massive viewership figures in 2026, where advertising generated over $1.1 billion.

📈 The move signals a massive shift from linear cable to digital streaming in sports broadcasting. Will Netflix take over major sports rights permanently?
