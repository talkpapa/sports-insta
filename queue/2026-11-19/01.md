Leicester City have officially terminated their front-of-shirt sponsorship deal with BC Game following public backlash.

🎰 The agreement with Belize-registered crypto casino BC Game was originally scheduled to run through the end of the current season. However, tension erupted after the sponsor named adult film actor Eva Elfie as a new global brand ambassador, leading the Foxes Trust to raise serious ethical concerns over gambling and adult content association.

👕 Citing differing commercial priorities, Leicester moved quickly to break the contract early. To appease affected supporters, the club announced that fans who purchased jerseys bearing BC Game branding within the last 28 days are eligible for a full exchange or refund, including standard delivery return postage.

🏛️ This commercial separation comes at a turbulent time off the pitch, as Leicester's ownership group continues to evaluate a potential sale of the club alongside their stadium and training facilities.
