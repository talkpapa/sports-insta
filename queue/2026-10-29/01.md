Chelsea have handed Pedro Neto a new contract with improved terms after the winger rejected summer interest from Manchester City.

📝 Pedro Neto has committed his long-term future to Chelsea by signing a new five-year contract with an option for a further year. Despite summer interest from Manchester City and other Premier League clubs, the Portuguese international chose to remain at Stamford Bridge to play under head coach Xabi Alonso.

💰 Chelsea's ownership strategy relies on long-term contracts, but hierarchy continues to offer improved terms as a reward for top performances and loyalty. While former teammate Enzo Fernández was sold to City for £125m following transfer requests and internal friction, Neto's pay rise sends a clear message about squad culture.

⚽ Adapted into an attacking wingback role by Alonso, the 26-year-old has already scored twice in five appearances this season. The deal follows similar renewals for key squad members like Cole Palmer and Moisés Caicedo as Chelsea look to solidify their core squad.
