Burnley gamble on former Basel manager Fabio Celestini to salvage their Championship campaign after sacking Nicky Hayen.

🚨 The Clarets have endured a nightmare start following Premier League relegation, winning just one of their opening 12 league fixtures. Sacking Hayen after four months, the hierarchy acted fast to halt a catastrophic slide towards League One.

📊 Celestini arrived after leading Basel from the bottom of the Swiss Super League to a league and cup double in just 18 months. New chief football officer Diego López prioritized his demonstrated ability to fix broken dressing rooms and adapt tactics rapidly.

⚽ Celestini takes charge immediately ahead of Saturday's home fixture against Portsmouth, aiming to instil instant belief in a deeply underperforming squad.

Can Celestini save Burnley from a disastrous season?
