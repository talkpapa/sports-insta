Liverpool paid £123m to land Bradley Barcola after PSG shifted tactical directions in attack. ⚽ Liverpool have completed the signing of French forward Bradley Barcola from Paris Saint-Germain in a blockbuster transaction. The 22-year-old fulfilled a childhood ambition by moving to Anfield after finding himself squeezed out of the starting lineup in Paris. 📊 The turning point occurred following the arrival of Khvicha Kvaratskhelia at PSG, which severely limited Barcola's starting opportunities. Inability to adapt effectively to the right flank left him on the bench for European finals, prompting a departure that netted PSG nearly €100m in pure profit. 💡 For Liverpool, the move secures a long-term target capable of exploiting space on the wing. PSG total summer sales exceeded €350m, setting a record for Ligue 1 while granting Barcola his preferred Premier League destination. Can Barcola establish himself as a starter at Anfield this season?
