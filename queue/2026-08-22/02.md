🚨 Gary Neville believes signing Julian Alvarez is the final piece to turn Arsenal into Champions League winners.

🎯 According to Sky Sports, pundit Gary Neville has urged Arsenal to make a bold move for Atletico Madrid's forward in the final ten days of the transfer window. Neville argues that while Mikel Arteta has built an elite squad, they still require a bit of magic up front to conquer Europe.

💰 Getting him out of Madrid is almost impossible, though. Atletico Madrid CEO Miguel Angel Gil has previously stated the club has no intention of selling the Argentine international, even if an astronomical bid of £170 million were to arrive.
