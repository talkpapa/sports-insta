Tottenham are on the verge of a sensational £160m double swoop for Manchester City's Savinho and Omar Marmoush.

💰 Manchester City are set to bank a massive profit. Savinho, who joined the City Football Group for £30.8m, is heading to North London for £85m after requesting a transfer. Marmoush will follow for £75m, securing City a tidy return on the £59m they paid Frankfurt.

🔄 Manager Enzo Maresca admitted that keeping unhappy players is nearly impossible, drawing comparisons to Rodri's departure. With Marmoush also finding his playing time heavily limited by Erling Haaland, City decided that cashing in on both players was the most sensible route.

⚪ This double deal will take Tottenham's summer spending under Roberto De Zerbi to a staggering £387m. Following big-money moves for Sandro Tonali and Mateus Fernandes, Spurs are fully backing their manager to challenge at the very top.
