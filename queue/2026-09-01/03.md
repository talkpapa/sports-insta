🚨 Manchester City have agreed a £65m deal for Everton's Iliman Ndiaye after a wild sequence of transfer collapses.

📦 City will pay a guaranteed £60m plus £5m in add-ons for the 26-year-old Senegal winger. This move only became reality after Pep Guardiola's side saw their £80m pursuit of Liverpool's Cody Gakpo collapse, as the Anfield club could not secure a replacement in time before the deadline.

🔄 Everton had actually agreed to sell Ndiaye to Tottenham first. However, that deal was heavily dependent on Richarlison moving the other way in a separate £35m transfer. When the Brazilian forward failed to agree on personal terms with his former club, the Spurs deal fell apart completely.

⏱️ With the transfer window closing rapidly, City jumped on the opportunity to secure Ndiaye, while Tottenham have now turned their attention to Mykhailo Mudryk on loan from Chelsea.
