🚨 Monaco’s CEO has revealed the extraordinary reason why Folarin Balogun's £40m move to Everton collapsed on deadline day.

📦 According to Monaco chief Thiago Scuro, both clubs had fully signed the paperwork after Everton initially raised medical concerns. However, the striker felt deeply insulted by how Everton’s medical department questioned his long-term health, especially after those doubts were leaked to the media within thirty minutes.

💼 The dispute caused a massive domino effect. Monaco had agreed to sell Lamine Camara to Chelsea for £47m to raise funds, but they pulled out of that deal once Everton proceeded with Balogun. When Balogun then refused to sign, Monaco lost out on both transfers.
