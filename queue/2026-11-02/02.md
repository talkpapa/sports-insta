Arsenal sanction a record-breaking £60m transfer as Gabriel Martinelli moves to Saudi Pro League side Al-Hilal.

🚨 Arsenal have officially authorized the departure of Gabriel Martinelli to Al-Hilal for a club-record £60m fee, surpassing their previous record of £40m set when selling Alex Oxlade-Chamberlain to Liverpool in 2017.

💼 The 25-year-old winger saw his role reduced at the Emirates despite contributing to last season's title-winning campaign. Reports indicate the agreement contains a sell-on clause, while Martinelli secures a four-year deal earning approximately £17m per season.

🔄 The Gunners decided against bringing in a late replacement before the deadline, following earlier exits for Gabriel Jesus and Fábio Vieira.
