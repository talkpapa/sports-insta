💸 Tottenham spent £300m but sit bottom of the Premier League. Here is why their record rebuild collapsed so quickly.

📉 Despite handing Roberto De Zerbi the biggest transfer war chest in club history, Spurs are suffering their worst start in 18 years. Financial Fair Play restrictions prevented De Zerbi from landing his priority left-winger and stopped the club from clearing out squad deadwood.

🛑 The recruitment strategy also stripped the squad of its spine. Selling Cristian Romero and losing combative midfielder Joao Palhinha left a massive leadership void. Without them, Tottenham have failed to win a single match when conceding first for nearly two years.

🔄 Compounding the issue, tactical gamble Jan Paul van Hecke has struggled to replace the departed defenders, while academy graduate Luka Vuskovic is thriving elsewhere. De Zerbi now has a highly expensive squad that lacks the natural leaders needed to survive a Premier League slump.

Can De Zerbi turn this around, or did Spurs waste £300m?
