🚨 Pep Guardiola has revealed that his next role in management will be on the international stage rather than with another club.

⏱️ Speaking in an interview, the manager explained he turned down an offer from Italy because he needs personal time away from football. The less intense schedule of national team coaching offers a way back into the game without the daily pressure of top-tier club management.

🇺🇸 While Mauricio Pochettino has publicly encouraged him to consider the USMNT job, Guardiola stressed that he remains in no rush and has not set a strict timeline for his comeback.
