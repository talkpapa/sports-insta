Arsenal's decision to hold back on a marquee striker could leave Mikel Arteta short after letting key forwards depart.

⚽ The Gunners began the summer with strong additions in Christos Tzolis, Bruno Guimarães, and Ezri Konsa. However, their search for a elite forward stalled when initial target Morgan Rogers joined Chelsea, forcing a pivot to Vinícius Júnior and Julián Alvarez that ultimately yielded no deal.

💰 Rather than resorting to a deadline-day panic buy, the club chose financial restraint. This leaves the squad light after approving exits for Gabriel Jesus to Barcelona and Ethan Nwaneri to Borussia Dortmund, with Gabriel Martinelli also attracting interest from Al-Hilal.

🚨 Without a new headline attacker to lead the line, any squad injury could severely strain their campaign. Did Arsenal make the right call waiting for the right player, or will this gamble cost them?
