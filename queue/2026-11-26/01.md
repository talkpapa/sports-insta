Alexander Isak is finally showing why Liverpool invested £125m to bring him from Newcastle.

⚽ Alexander Isak's debut year at Liverpool was derailed before it even started. A protracted transfer battle meant he arrived without a pre-season, and a severe leg fracture in December capped off a frustrating four-goal season.

📈 The turn of form in his second season comes down to physical readiness and tactical integration. Under Andoni Iraola, Isak is not only scoring—reaching 10 goals by November—but also executing the deep pressing and defensive duties required by the coaching staff.

🔥 With eight away goals already across all competitions, he has matched Erling Haaland for the most on the road this term and outscored Liverpool's entire squad away output from last season.

Can Isak lead Liverpool to silverware this season?
