🚨 Nottingham Forest are closing in on Chelsea's Liam Delap as they look to secure a major late-window signing.

According to reports, Forest are in advanced negotiations for the 23-year-old striker. Delap originally chose Chelsea over Forest when leaving Ipswich, but his tenure at Stamford Bridge has quickly stalled.

The forward has been frozen out of the Chelsea squad, training away from the main group and completely omitted from the recent Fulham match. This availability has opened the door for Forest, who have long tracked the player.

Forest manager Oliver Glasner has publicly demanded two more high-quality additions before the transfer deadline. With a £34m deal for Ousmane Diomande already secured this summer, landing Delap would represent another significant investment in Forest's squad.
