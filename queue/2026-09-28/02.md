🇺🇸 Manchester City’s future signing Cavan Sullivan is making waves after making his senior USA debut at just 16. The Philadelphia Union midfielder became the fourth youngest player in USMNT history during a 4-1 win over Peru. Since City secured his future signature in 2024 when he was just 14, his development has been highly anticipated. Sullivan is backing up the hype with elite MLS production, recording seven goals and five assists this season. He has inspired an 11-game unbeaten run for Philadelphia, scoring six goals in his last six matches. However, a complication looms in the background. His debut occurred just one day after City were found guilty of breaching the majority of their 115 financial rules, which the club is expected to appeal. Will he become the global star the US has been waiting for?
