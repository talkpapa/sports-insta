🚨 Europe’s biggest leagues have launched a direct attack on FIFA’s leadership, calling its current governance "not fit for purpose."

The Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, and the Bundesliga have united in a fierce demand for structural reform. They accuse FIFA President Gianni Infantino of promoting "exploitative ideas" that threaten the global game, pointing to constant calendar expansion like the new Club World Cup, which directly strains club squads and domestic competitions.

To strip FIFA of its absolute monopoly, the leagues are demanding three major changes. They want a permanent decision-making body that includes clubs, leagues, and player representatives, complete transparency in major decision-making, and a strict separation of FIFA's regulatory duties from its commercial operations.

With Infantino currently running unopposed for a fourth term, the leagues are demanding all presidential candidates commit to these reforms within their first 100 days.
