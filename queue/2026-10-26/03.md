Arsenal compiled a shortlist of over 20 players before securing key summer deals. Here is how their window unfolded.

📋 Arsenal entered the summer window with ambitious plans to build on their title success, holding a shortlist featuring over 20 targets across Europe. However, early targets like Elliot Anderson opted for Manchester City, while Sandro Tonali joined Tottenham for £92m and Amadou Onana suffered a serious knee injury.

💼 That forced sporting director Andrea Berta to pivot towards Bruno Guimaraes. Using intermediary networks, Arsenal sealed a £75m move for the Newcastle star without early direct club negotiations. Defensive reinforcement was also secured with Ezri Konsa arriving from Villa for £51m plus £4m in add-ons after an initial £40m bid was rejected.

🎯 The strategy delivered key additions tailored for Mikel Arteta's starting XI, even as some fans felt unfulfilled by missed targets.
