Arsenal left fans stunned by signing no late forwards on deadline day after offloading Gabriel Martinelli and Gabriel Jesus.

⚽ Mikel Arteta wanted squad depth, but Arsenal spent months pursuing ambitious dream targets like Vinícius Júnior and Julián Álvarez rather than settling for squad fillers. When late targets like Malick Fofana emerged, Arteta reportedly doubted whether they would truly elevate the squad.

💰 Offloading Gabriel Martinelli to Al-Hilal for £60m alongside Gabriel Jesus's exit slashed Arsenal's net spend to just £78m this window. While rivals spent heavily, the champions prioritized financial balance after earlier deals for Bruno Guimarães, Eberechi Eze, Ezri Konsa, and Christos Tzolis.

📋 The club is now betting on tactical continuity and current depth across four competitions, relying heavily on Bukayo Saka and their existing rotation.

Will Arsenal's decision to trust existing squad depth pay off in the title race?
