Manchester United is selling 7x7cm cubes of Old Trafford turf for £125 as debt remains over £1bn. 🏟️ In a highly unusual move, Manchester United has packaged pieces of the historic Old Trafford pitch into glass cubes. Dug up for the first time in 14 years, the turf is being marketed as a premium memento, but the timing of the release has drawn intense scrutiny. 📉 The announcement coincided with financial reports showing overall club debt still exceeds £1bn. Despite bringing in a record revenue of £677.6m and a £22.6m operating profit for the 2025-26 period, the club ultimately posted a pre-tax loss of £43m. 🏗️ This drive for extra revenue comes as United plans for the future. The club confirmed it has already spent £63.5m on land acquisitions for a proposed new stadium, a project estimated to cost upwards of £2bn overall.
