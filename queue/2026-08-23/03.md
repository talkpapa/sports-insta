🔴 Manchester United need a left-back to support Luke Shaw, but INEOS’s strict budget rules have stalled a deal for Lewis Hall.

Luke Shaw started every Premier League match last season, but his long history of injuries means Manchester United cannot rely on him to play 50-plus matches as Champions League football returns to Old Trafford. With alternative options played out of position, manager Michael Carrick has made a new left-back his absolute priority.

The top target is Newcastle's 21-year-old defender Lewis Hall. However, negotiations have hit a wall over Newcastle’s hefty £70 million valuation, which Sir Jim Ratcliffe and the INEOS-led recruitment team refuse to match in an effort to maintain strict cost control.

While United are close to completing a midfield deal for Brighton's Carlos Baleba, the squad remains dangerously thin at the back. United must now decide whether to abandon their pursuit of Hall for cheaper alternatives or risk starting a grueling European campaign without adequate defensive cover.
