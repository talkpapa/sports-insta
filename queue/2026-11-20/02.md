Tottenham broke transfer records in Ireland to sign 18-year-old Mason Melia before sending him straight to Lincoln City. ⚽ Despite his young age, Melia had already made almost 100 first-team appearances for St Patrick's Athletic. That rare level of senior experience for an 18-year-old triggered intense interest from clubs across Europe and the Football League. 📊 Tottenham won the race by paying a League of Ireland record fee for the forward. Melia offers rare tactical versatility, capable of leading the line as a focal point or dropping deep to link play and finish chances. 🎯 With Lincoln struggling for efficiency in front of goal after managing just one shot on target from 18 attempts recently, the loan spell guarantees Melia vital competitive minutes while Spurs monitor his development. Will Mason Melia make the step up to Tottenham's first team after his EFL loan spell?
