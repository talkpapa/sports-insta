📌 Arsenal raised £60m selling Gabriel Martinelli but brought in zero attackers on deadline day. Here is why.

🎯 Mikel Arteta and sporting director Andrea Berta focused their transfer efforts on elite targets like Vinícius Júnior. When those deals proved unfeasible, Arteta chose not to sign secondary squad fillers like Malick Fofana, preferring squad continuity over short-term fixes.

💰 Offloading high earner Gabriel Jesus and selling Martinelli to Al-Hilal dramatically reduced Arsenal's net spend to just £78m. Despite earlier additions like Bruno Guimarães and Ezri Konsa, the decision leaves Arsenal with a slim attack across four competitions.
