Arsenal have smashed their all-time sales record by selling Gabriel Martinelli to Al-Hilal for £60m. 🚨 The Brazilian winger leaves North London after falling down the pecking order despite winning the Premier League title last season. The £60m fee surpasses Arsenal's previous record sale of £40m for Alex Oxlade-Chamberlain to Liverpool in 2017. 💼 Financial details reveal a lucrative move for the player, who signed a four-year contract in Saudi Arabia worth approximately £17m per season. Arsenal also protected future upside by inserting a sell-on clause into the final agreement. 🔄 Martinelli becomes the latest departure in a busy exit window for Arsenal, who also sanctioned moves for Gabriel Jesus and Fábio Vieira without bringing in deadline-day replacements.
