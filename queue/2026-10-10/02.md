📈 England's top clubs are hoarding Europe's best teenagers, creating an insular market only they can afford.

💸 Premier League clubs have signed 100 teenagers over the last five years, averaging 20 per season compared to La Liga's average of just 5.8. English teams are paying premium fees for raw potential, spending £20m on Sean Steur after just 20 senior games, and an incredible £81.5m on Manchester City's Ayyoub Bouaddi.

🔄 Because continental clubs cannot match Premier League salaries or valuations, English clubs must trade almost exclusively among themselves. This keeps elite talent locked within the division, further concentrating power in England.

⚖️ Financial rules like squad cost ratios also incentivize these domestic swaps. This keeps money circulating internally, turning the Premier League into a self-sustaining ecosystem that shuts out the rest of Europe.
