🇪🇨 From the "next Maradona" to Chelsea's U21s. What happened to Kendry Paez?

When Chelsea secured the Ecuadorian playmaker for £17.27m at age 16, he looked like a generational talent. But now aged 19, his career has stalled. After struggles with form and off-pitch attitude led to terminated loan spells at both Strasbourg and River Plate, he returned to London out of favor with new manager Xabi Alonso.

Chelsea wanted to send him on another developmental loan to rebuild his confidence, but hit a regulatory wall. FIFA rules restrict clubs to just six international loan departures per season—and Chelsea had already exhausted their limit. A domestic loan in England also failed to materialise.

As a result, Paez has been registered as an academy player and made his debut for the Under-21 side. While his camp believes the concerns are overstated, the young midfielder must now rebuild his reputation from the youth ranks.

Can Paez turn his career around at Chelsea?
