Major League Soccer is rewriting its own financial rules, triggering a massive 65% surge in summer transfer spending.

💰 Traditional MLS transfers used to rely heavily on General Allocation Money, a complex internal currency. A recent rule change allows clubs to buy players using direct cash transfer fees, complementing the old system instead of replacing it.

🔄 This adjustment allows teams to convert up to $3 million of their transfer revenue into GAM annually. Because hitting this cap is now much easier, teams have vastly more internal currency to buy down salary cap hits and execute domestic trades.

📈 Combined with a booming youth academy pipeline that has generated nearly $800 million in outbound sales since 2022, MLS franchises now possess unprecedented financial flexibility ahead of the 2026 World Cup.
