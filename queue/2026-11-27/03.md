🚨 Scotland's ambitious plan to safeguard domestic youth pathways has hit a major hurdle after clubs requested further consultation. 📊 The proposed framework aimed to force SPFL clubs into giving Scottish U21 players an average of 60 minutes per match across all divisions, using a system of financial bonuses and fines to enforce compliance. The move comes as Scottish Premiership minutes for local youngsters plummet and English academies actively recruit top Scottish prospects before they reach senior football. ⏱️ But with managers hesitant to compromise match results and clubs demanding diluted terms, the SFA pushed the member vote back to early 2027 ahead of a planned 2027-28 implementation.
