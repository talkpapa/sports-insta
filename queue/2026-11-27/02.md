Brighton signed Moisés Caicedo and Carlos Baleba for under £30m combined, then turned them into £180m in transfer profits.

📌 Brighton's transfer strategy relies on Jamestown Analytics, a data firm created by owner Tony Bloom. The proprietary algorithm scans lesser-known leagues to identify undervalued players tailored to specific tactical roles before rivals notice them.

💰 Instead of spending huge fees on proven stars, the club buys cheap raw potential. When premier clubs pay massive sums for their top talent, Brighton immediately plug the gap with pre-identified replacements, keeping squad quality high and balance sheets green.

📊 Can this data-led recruitment model eventually propel a mid-table spender to a Premier League title?
