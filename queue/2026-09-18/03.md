🚨 Real Madrid are leading the chase for Manchester United's 15-year-old prodigy JJ Gabriel, who wants out immediately.

😮 The highly-rated forward has formally requested to cancel his registration at Old Trafford. His camp reportedly feels United broke promises regarding a fast-tracked pathway to the first team, pointing to how other English clubs blood 15-year-olds in senior football.

🇪🇸 Real Madrid's plan is to immediately place Gabriel into Real Madrid Castilla. Unlike English academies, Castilla plays in the Spanish third division, offering genuine senior competitive football, which highly appeals to the teenager's entourage.

✍️ Manchester United are fighting to block the immediate exit, claiming his registration runs until the end of the season. However, because Gabriel holds an EU passport, he is legally permitted to sign for an EU-based club like Real Madrid as soon as he turns 16 on October 6.
