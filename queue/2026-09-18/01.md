🇵🇹 Pedro Neto has committed his future to Chelsea with an improved contract, proving that the club's long-term wage structure is highly flexible. While Chelsea's hierarchy is famous for signing players to lengthy deals, they are using immediate pay rises to reward on-pitch success and loyalty. Neto, who joined for £54m, chose to stay and play under Xabi Alonso despite intense summer interest from Manchester City. This stands in stark contrast to Enzo Fernández, who was sold to City for £125m after his relationship with teammates fractured.
