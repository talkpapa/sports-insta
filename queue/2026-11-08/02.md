⚡ Brentford shattered their transfer record by spending £39m on Malian midfielder Mamadou Sangaré after his rapid rise in France.🧠 The 24-year-old's valuation exploded after starring at Lens, where he won the Marc-Vivien Foé award as Ligue 1's top African player while lifting the Coupe de France. Brentford scouts identified his elite press resistance, composure under pressure, and relentless work rate as the perfect fit for their central midfield gap.📊 Sangaré made an immediate splash on his Premier League debut against Tottenham, delivering an assist and commanding the engine room. His journey from Mali to Austria, France, and England highlights one of the sharpest player recruitments of the window.Can Sangaré establish himself among the Premier League's elite midfielders this season?
