⚽ The son of Ballon d'Or winner Andriy Shevchenko has dropped to the seventh tier to rebuild his career. Nineteen-year-old Kristian Shevchenko has signed a permanent contract with Sholing FC after his release from Watford's academy. Sholing's coaching staff used their academy connections to secure the former Chelsea and Tottenham youth player. Sholing is executing a deliberate transfer strategy of recruiting elite academy castaways. They plan to give these technical youngsters physical, non-league first-team experience to help them earn contracts back in the professional leagues.
