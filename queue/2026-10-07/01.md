Why did Liverpool refuse to spend their way out of an early-season injury crisis? Here is the strategy.

💰 Despite recording the Premier League's highest net spend after adding Bradley Barcola for a potential £123m, Liverpool chose not to buy emergency cover at right-back, defensive midfield, or right-wing.

🤕 With Hugo Ekitiké, Conor Bradley, and Giovanni Leoni sidelined, head coach Andoni Iraola defended the decision, stating that paying "crazy money" for two or three months of cover makes no economic sense.

🔄 Instead, the club will rely on the versatility of Barcola, Cody Gakpo, Victor Muñoz, and Rio Ngumoha to cover both flanks until their long-term options return to full fitness.
