🔵 Why Brighton paid £12.5m for a Championship winger with deceptive stats.

Brighton’s recruitment team is famous for spotting undervalued talent, and their deadline day move for Femi Azeez fits the template perfectly. Reportedly chased by the club for a while, the winger completed his move for an initial £12.5m, which could eventually rise to £15m with add-ons.

While his raw tally of seven assists last season might look modest, his underlying metrics paint a completely different picture. Azeez ranked first in the Championship for open-play crosses with 227 and generated an expected assists rating of 9.12. He also ranked fourth in the league for dribble attempts with 131 and created 18 big chances.

The massive gap between his expected assists and his actual output suggests his Millwall teammates simply failed to convert his deliveries. By placing him alongside Brighton's higher-quality finishers, the club hopes to instantly unlock his true creative value at the Premier League level.

Can Azeez make the step up to the top flight?
