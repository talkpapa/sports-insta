Ollie Watkins has completed a £51m move to Saudi club Al-Hilal, ending his historic Aston Villa career.

🇸🇦 The 30-year-old striker has signed a three-year contract with Al-Hilal. Watkins, who scored 91 Premier League goals for Aston Villa, admitted he never intended for his time at the club to end under controversial circumstances after making himself unavailable for their opening match.

📉 His departure is part of a massive summer clear-out at Villa Park. The club has parted ways with several key players, including Emiliano Martinez and Lucas Digne, to strictly comply with UEFA's squad cost ratio regulations after breaking their own transfer record twice.

🔄 Money from the sale is already being reinvested, with Villa closing in on a £29m deal for defender Taylor Harwood-Bellis and having already secured striker Nicolas Jackson for £65m.
