Chelsea have agreed a surprise £7.5m deal for Aston Villa goalkeeper Emiliano Martinez after a sudden change of heart.

🧤 Just days ago, Chelsea's hierarchy intended to back Robert Sanchez as their undisputed number one. However, his high-profile blunders against Fulham forced an immediate reassessment, prompting a late-window swoop for Villa's veteran keeper.

🤝 The bargain £7.5m fee reflects Aston Villa's readiness to sell. Having already brought in Japan international Zion Suzuki for £30m, Villa were happy to let the 33-year-old leave, especially given the clubs' history of highly cooperative transfer business.

🏥 Martinez is now set for a medical in London, hoping to put a previously failed Juventus move behind him. Chelsea also have teenager Mike Penders incoming, but Martinez provides the immediate Premier League experience they desperately need.
