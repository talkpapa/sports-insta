🔴 An incredible piece of Liverpool history has found a new home after a massive bidding war. The original "This Is Anfield" sign, commissioned by legendary manager Bill Shankly in 1972, has sold for a final total of £149,500 including commission and VAT after being estimated at just £15,000. 📜 This is the actual physical sign that hung in the tunnel during Liverpool's golden era, including their back-to-back European Cup triumphs in 1977 and 1978. Shankly famously rejected a "Welcome to Anfield" design because he wanted to remind his players who they represented, while terrifying opponents. 🏨 After being replaced in the late 1970s, former manager Roy Evans gifted the sign to ex-youth player Ronald Wilson. His family kept it displayed in an Isle of Man hotel for over 20 years before it finally went under the hammer this week. Would you pay six figures for a piece of your club's history?
