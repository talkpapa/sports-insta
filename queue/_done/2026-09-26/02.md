🔴 Bradley Barcola has completed a sensational £123m move to Liverpool, fulfilling a childhood dream.

🇫🇷 From dreaming of Anfield at age 15 to becoming Liverpool's latest marquee signing, Bradley Barcola's move represents a massive statement of intent. The Premier League side lands one of France's most exciting attacking outlets to bolster their frontline.

📉 The transfer became logical after PSG signed Khvicha Kvaratskhelia in January 2025. With Barcola struggling to impact games from the right wing or through the center, his minutes on his preferred left flank vanished, leading him to seek an exit despite holding a contract until 2028.

💰 For PSG, the business is historic. Having signed Barcola for €45m from Lyon, they net a profit of nearly €100m. This deal makes Barcola their most expensive departure ever, eclipsing Neymar's sale and pushing PSG's summer departures to a record €350m.

Can Barcola secure a permanent starting spot in Liverpool's competitive frontline?
