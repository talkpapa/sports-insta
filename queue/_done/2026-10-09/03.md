📉 Manchester United spent just £163m this summer, choosing squad depth over a single blockbuster signing.

📊 While Tottenham went big by spending £334m—including a massive £100m move for Sandro Tonali under Roberto De Zerbi—United took a very different path. Instead of hunting for one superstar midfielder, the board decided they needed to rebuild the spine of the squad by spreading their resources across three separate signings.

💸 Youri Tielemans, Andrey Santos, and Carlos Baleba all arrived to bolster the midfield. This strategy was driven by necessity: United's struggle to generate high-value player sales meant they could not afford both a massive individual transfer and the squad depth needed for a grueling multi-competition season.
