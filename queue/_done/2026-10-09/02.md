Arsenal's late-window gamble leaves Mikel Arteta with a remarkably thin attacking line for the long season ahead.

🔴 The Premier League champions spent their summer chasing blockbuster targets like Vinícius Júnior and Julián Alvarez, but ended up signing none of them. Instead, they let Gabriel Jesus leave and sold Gabriel Martinelli to Al-Hilal for £60m on deadline day without adding attacking depth.

📈 Mikel Arteta ultimately vetoed late moves, including a potential deal for Malick Fofana, because he doubted they would genuinely elevate the squad. The club preferred keeping a tight, elite roster rather than spending just to fill numbers, resulting in a net spend of £78m.

⚙️ While the arrivals of Bruno Guimarães, Christos Tzolis, and Ezri Konsa provide strong cover in midfield and defence, the forward line looks incredibly light. This places an immense burden on Bukayo Saka after his recent injury-hit campaigns.

Was Arsenal right to reject squad fillers in favor of waiting for world-class talent?
