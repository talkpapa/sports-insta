Tottenham could trim their squad before Friday's Turkish deadline as Richarlison prepares for a move to Trabzonspor.

⚽ While the English transfer window closed on Tuesday, Turkey's market remains open until Friday night. Tottenham manager Roberto De Zerbi revealed that Richarlison asked to leave multiple times this summer, having previously turned down a move back to Everton.

📊 Spurs currently hold 18 non-homegrown senior players on their books, which is one over the Premier League squad allowance. Moving the 29-year-old forward solves this registration conflict while clearing wages for a player entering his final contract year.

🇹🇷 Trabzonspor have already agreed personal terms with the Brazilian after a rough start to their domestic and European campaign. The Turkish side is now working to finalize terms with Spurs before the deadline.
