🇧🇷 Tottenham could be set to part ways with Richarlison as the Turkish transfer window enters its final hours.

According to manager Roberto De Zerbi, the Brazilian forward has requested a transfer multiple times this summer. Despite turning down a return to Everton, reports state he has now agreed personal terms with Turkish side Trabzonspor, who are desperate for reinforcements after a poor start to their campaign.

For Spurs, the sale solves a pressing registration crisis. The club currently has 18 senior non-homegrown players on their books, which is one more than Premier League rules permit. With Richarlison entering the final year of his contract, letting him leave now prevents a squad headache and avoids losing him for free next summer.
