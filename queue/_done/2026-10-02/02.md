🚨 Folarin Balogun's £40m move to Everton collapsed at the final second—and it cost Chelsea a £47m signing too. According to Monaco CEO Thiago Scuro, Everton's medical department raised late, unfounded concerns about the USMNT striker's fitness, which leaked to the media within 30 minutes. Feeling insulted and uncomfortable with how he was treated, Balogun refused to sign his contract despite the club's late rush to finalize the paperwork. Everton sources defend their stance, claiming the player arrived late and Monaco delayed sending medical records, leaving them with no time to assess a physical issue. The chaos triggered a brutal domino effect: Monaco, needing to sell just one player for financial reasons, pulled the plug on Lamine Camara's £47m move to Chelsea once Everton initially agreed to proceed with Balogun.
