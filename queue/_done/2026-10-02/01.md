⚖️ Manchester City are risking it all on a highly complex legal defense to overturn their financial rule breaches. The club is attempting to prove that £830.69m in disputed sponsorship money actually came from the Abu Dhabi government rather than their owner's company, ADUG. However, an independent commission has already dismissed this defense once, calling it a fabricated story designed to hide a disguised funding scheme. To buy more time, City's legal team is also arguing against new Premier League rules that force appeals to conclude within 12 weeks. They argue that because they were charged under older regulations, they should not be subjected to the speeded-up process, potentially dragging this battle deep into next year. Will this high-risk strategy save Manchester City?
