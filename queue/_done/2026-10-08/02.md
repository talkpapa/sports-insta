Arsenal are ready to make Declan Rice one of their highest-paid players with a major contract extension.

💰 According to reports, negotiations are at an advanced stage to extend the 27-year-old's stay beyond next season. The proposed deal will reportedly elevate his weekly salary from £240,000 to around £350,000, putting him on par with star winger Bukayo Saka.

🏆 Since his £105m move from West Ham in 2023, the England international has become indispensable, helping end the club's 22-year league title drought last term. Keeping him happy is a top priority for the Gunners' leadership.

🤝 This breakthrough comes right after manager Mikel Arteta signed a new four-year contract. Arsenal are aggressively locking down the key pillars of their champion squad to ensure their dominant cycle continues.
