🇺🇸 MLS has completely transformed its transfer market, with summer spending skyrocketing by 65% year-over-year.

Historically, MLS clubs were heavily restricted by complex roster regulations and internal currency systems like General Allocation Money (GAM). However, the introduction of the new "cash-for-player" rule has unlocked a massive wave of spending. This mechanism bypassed previous limitations, accounting for $41 million in transfers that previously would have been impossible to complete.

This rule change also feeds back into the league's internal economy. Clubs can convert up to $3 million in outbound transfer revenue into GAM each year. Because MLS teams are selling younger academy talents for record fees—generating nearly $800 million in outbound sales since 2022—they now have more internal currency than ever to buy down salary cap hits.
