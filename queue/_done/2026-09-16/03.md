🇧🇷 Philippe Coutinho and Neymar are finally teammates at club level after a surprise move to Santos.

After returning to boyhood club Vasco da Gama in 2024, Coutinho struggled with the immense pressure, leaving the club in February after 81 appearances due to feeling mentally tired. As a free agent, the former £142m Barcelona midfielder decided to prioritize a fresh start.

This short-term deal represents the first time the close friends will play together for the same club, following Neymar's own return to Santos in January last year. The club celebrated the signing by sharing nostalgia-filled media of the pair re-enacting teenage photos.

Can this Brazilian reunion help Coutinho find his joy for football again?
