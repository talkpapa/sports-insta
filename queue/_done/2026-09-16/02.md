📉 From Spurs starter to Eredivisie redemption: why Ajax is taking a short-term gamble on free agent Yves Bissouma.

🤝 According to reports, Yves Bissouma is currently in Amsterdam to finalise a move to Ajax. The 30-year-old midfielder has been without a club since his release from Tottenham in June, and this initial agreement is understood to run until the end of the current season.

⚠️ The transfer represents a low-risk gamble for the Dutch giants. Bissouma’s final year in North London was highly turbulent; he was excluded from the UEFA Super Cup squad in August 2025 by manager Thomas Frank due to persistent lateness, eventually making just 11 Premier League appearances as Spurs finished 17th.

✍️ To protect themselves, Ajax has structured the contract carefully. The short-term deal features a club option to extend his stay for an additional 12 months, giving them ample time to evaluate if the former Brighton star can rediscover his best form without committing to a massive long-term financial burden.
