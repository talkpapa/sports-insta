English clubs are buying up Europe's best young talent long before they hit their peak years. Premier League teams are spending massive fees on unproven 18-year-olds with only a handful of senior games. Over the last five seasons, English top-flight clubs completed 100 permanent signings of teenagers, far outstripping La Liga's 29 and Ligue 1's 47. Because England hoards elite prospects early, the global talent pool is increasingly concentrated inside the Premier League. Combined with squad cost rules encouraging player trading, English clubs are now building an insular domestic market where they mostly buy from each other.
