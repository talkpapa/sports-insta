Pedro Neto just bagged an upgraded Chelsea deal after snubbing Manchester City—here is why the club broke their usual routine.

💰 Reports indicate Manchester City chased the Portuguese winger during the summer window. While Chelsea sold Enzo Fernández to City for £125m after transfer friction, Neto chose to stay, proving his commitment to the ongoing project.

📋 Chelsea’s ownership model relies on long, incentivised contracts, but management wants to show that top performers will still receive pay bumps without needing to threaten an exit.
