🚨 AFC Wimbledon's proposed £87m investment deal has collapsed after lead backer Omar Mir abruptly pulled out.

🏟️ The fan-owned EFL club has been searching for external capital to help balance its books. Wimbledon recorded a £2.6m loss last season and still owes £7.5m in debt linked to their return to Plough Lane. To facilitate fresh funding, supporters voted last April to lower their voting rights threshold to 50.01%.

💼 Sloane Street Capital submitted an £87m proposal for 90% of the club's economic rights, fronted by tech businessman Omar Mir alongside former Chelsea defender Ryan Bertrand. However, Mir withdrew from the deal while fan voting was underway, citing a change in investment priorities following scrutiny from supporters regarding his background.
