Liverpool dropped massive fees on Isak, Wirtz, and Barcola—yet left key positions untouched this summer. Here is why.

🧠 Despite recording the Premier League's highest net spend after committing up to £123m for Bradley Barcola, £125m for Alexander Isak, and £116m for Florian Wirtz, Liverpool chose not to sign a dedicated right-winger, defensive midfielder, or right-back.

💰 Reports show the club explored moves for Brighton's Yankuba Minteh and Crystal Palace's Ismaïla Sarr, but hierarchy decided against overpaying for temporary solutions while players like Hugo Ekitiké and Conor Bradley recover from long-term injuries.

📋 Head coach Andoni Iraola publicly backed the strategy, confirming he intends to deploy his flexible attacking lineup across both flanks rather than burning capital on mid-tier squad fillers.
