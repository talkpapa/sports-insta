👟 Kylian Mbappé has stunned the sportswear industry by ending his 20-year association with Nike to join Swiss brand On.

💼 This is more than a standard boot deal. Following the blueprint of tennis legend Roger Federer, Mbappé is becoming a shareholder in the Zurich-based company. This equity-based model gives him a direct financial stake in On's success as they prepare a massive push into football starting next year.

📈 The move is a significant blow to Nike, which previously lost young star Lamine Yamal to Adidas. On is entering a highly consolidated market where Nike and Adidas control over 40% of the market each, while challengers like Skechers and New Balance have historically struggled to gain major retail traction.

⚽ To lead this ambitious football expansion, On has also appointed French legend Thierry Henry as their football director. Mbappé will wear their new prototype boots as the brand expands from running and tennis into mainstream football apparel and training gear.

Can a challenger brand actually break the Nike and Adidas duopoly?
