Monaco and Crystal Palace triggered major deadline-day anger by blocking late transfers for Lamine Camara and Ismaïla Sarr.

🤝 Chelsea believed they had a secure £47m deal for Senegal midfielder Lamine Camara. However, Monaco's sporting director admitted they tore up the written agreement late on deadline night. The French side did so because they believed Folarin Balogun’s £40m move to Everton was back on, forcing them to quickly protect their squad depth.

❌ The move backfired completely. Balogun ultimately rejected the Everton switch due to unhappiness over his medical treatment, leaving Everton desperate and Monaco's relationship with Chelsea severely damaged. Meanwhile, Liverpool's late push for Palace forward Ismaïla Sarr also fell through after Palace refused to sell and Liverpool chose to retain Cody Gakpo.

🗣️ The chaotic handling of both Senegalese stars prompted agent Diomansy Kamara to speak out. He publicly accused clubs of treating African players like commodities, arguing their professional ambitions and personal wishes are completely ignored during high-stakes deadline day negotiations.
