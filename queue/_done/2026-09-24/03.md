🚨 Folarin Balogun’s £40m Everton move collapsed because he felt "mistreated" during his medical, Monaco's CEO claims.

🇫🇷 Monaco chief executive Thiago Scuro has blamed Everton's medical department for ruining the transfer. Balogun had completed medical tests, but Everton raised late concerns and reportedly leaked to the media that the striker was failing, causing immediate tension.

💰 Although Everton resolved their doubts and signed the contract paperwork just before the 11pm deadline, Balogun refused to sign his side of the deal. He allegedly felt insulted by the doubts cast on his health and chose to stay in Ligue 1.

🔄 The late collapse also wrecked Chelsea’s £47m move for Lamine Camara. Monaco had agreed to sell Camara to Stamford Bridge, but pulled the plug once Everton agreed to buy Balogun, only for Balogun's sudden rejection to leave Monaco with neither deal.
