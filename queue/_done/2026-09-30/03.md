Robbie Savage is on the verge of taking over at Peterborough United after Forest Green Rovers sanctioned his exit.

🚨 Forest Green chairman Dale Vince confirmed that the club granted permission for Savage to speak with Peterborough following a direct job offer. Savage personally requested to step down for personal reasons, which the club agreed to respect.

💬 Peterborough have been searching for a new head coach since parting ways with Luke Williams on September 19. Representatives from both clubs are currently negotiating the compensation fee to release the 51-year-old manager from his contract.

📈 Savage took charge of Forest Green in July 2025, guiding them to a seventh-place finish last term. He missed Forest Green's midweek fixture while exit negotiations were being finalised.
