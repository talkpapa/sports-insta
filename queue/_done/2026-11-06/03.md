Unai Emery is on the verge of signing a new contract with Aston Villa, securing his managerial role well past 2029.

🤝 According to official statements and reports, Villa chairman Nassef Sawiris and senior executives Roberto Olabe and Damian Vidagany met with Emery to finalize a strategic agreement. The extension expands the Spanish manager's influence over long-term sporting strategy and recruitment.

🏆 Since taking charge in 2022, Emery transformed Villa from relegation battlers into European trophy winners. Even after key players were sold during a difficult summer window, Villa remain competitive domestically and have taken three wins from four in the Champions League.

💼 By locking down Emery beyond his previous 2029 commitment, Villa also effectively shut down external interest from Premier League rivals seeking a world-class coach.
