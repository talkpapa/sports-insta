🚨 Harry Kane has effectively closed the door on returning to the Premier League by signing a new contract with Bayern Munich until 2029. ⚽ The 33-year-old striker extended his stay at the Allianz Arena following an extraordinary run of 102 goals in 102 Bundesliga games and a Ballon d'Or victory. The move locks in his peak years for Bayern while keeping him primed to lead England at Euro 2028. 🏆 By committing to Germany through his 35th birthday, Kane leaves his Premier League tally at 213 goals—47 short of Alan Shearer's legendary record of 260. Should Kane have returned to England to chase the all-time goal record, or did he make the right choice staying in Munich?
