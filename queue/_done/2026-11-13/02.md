🇫🇷 Bradley Barcola completes a £123m move from PSG to Liverpool, fulfilling a long-held childhood dream. 📉 The transfer follows a tactical shift in Paris where Barcola lost his primary starting role. After Khvicha Kvaratskhelia arrived in early 2025, Barcola was benched in major Champions League matches because he could not replicate his left-wing impact on the right flank or through the middle. 💰 Seeking guaranteed starts, Barcola pushed for Anfield. PSG accepted the massive bid to lock in a record profit of nearly €100m on the player, making him the most expensive departure in Ligue 1 history.
