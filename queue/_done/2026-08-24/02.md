⚖️ Jean-Philippe Mateta has lost his legal battle to tear up his Crystal Palace contract and force a transfer.

The French striker argued that Palace's unilateral activation of a 12-month extension was invalid. However, both an independent Premier League tribunal and FIFA rejected his claims, keeping him under contract until the end of this season.

Mateta has been pushing for an exit for some time, with reports linking him to Aston Villa as a potential replacement if Ollie Watkins moves to Saudi Arabia. His previous attempt to leave collapsed in January when he failed a medical with AC Milan ahead of a proposed £30m move.
