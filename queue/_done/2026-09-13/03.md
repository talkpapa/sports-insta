🔵 Manchester City just executed a historic £458m squad overhaul to prevent post-Pep Guardiola complacency.

🔄 With Guardiola departing after a decade of dominance, new manager Enzo Maresca faced a massive rebuilding task. The club bid farewell to legendary figures like Rodri, Bernardo Silva, and John Stones to completely reshape the squad's psychological dynamic.

💰 To power this transition, City spent a Premier League record £458 million on hungry talent including Enzo Fernandez, Elliot Anderson, and Ayyoub Bouaddi. Crucially, they offset this outlay by recouping over £300 million in outward transfers.

🗣️ New captain Ruben Dias revealed that the incoming players are desperate to win, perfectly matching the drive of the remaining core. With a perfect start to the campaign, Maresca's tactical ideas are already taking hold.

Can this newly rebuilt Manchester City squad defend their domestic dominance?
