🔄 Aston Villa just pulled off a massive £300m squad transformation to protect their long-term future.

Unai Emery's side completely overhauled their roster, moving on nine players to bring in ten fresh faces. Highly-rated prospects like Zion Suzuki, Ibrahim Mbaye, and Johan Manzambi arrived to radically alter the squad's profile and lower the overall wage bill after concerns the team had gone stale.

The financial mechanics are incredibly strategic. By generating £300 million in sales and capping incoming spend at £250 million, Villa pocketed a crucial surplus. This decisive action cleared the wage book and avoided the chaotic deadline-day scrambling that plagued them last year.

Can Villa maintain their top-tier momentum with such a drastically altered squad?
