🔵 Anthony Gordon is proving to be the tactical engine behind Hansi Flick’s explosive Barcelona side after a £70m move.

🇪🇸 When Barca spent £70m on the winger, many questioned the fee. Gordon had registered just six league goals for a struggling 12th-place Newcastle last season. However, sporting director Deco targeted him over Marcus Rashford because his profile perfectly matches Flick's aggressive tactical demands.

🎯 The Englishman has instantly transformed Barca's left wing with his selfless style. Instead of chasing individual glory, his space-stretching runs and elite off-the-ball movement have created endless opportunities, yielding four assists in his first five games.
