Clearlake Capital has bought out Todd Boehly and his partners to take absolute control of Chelsea.

🔵 The boardroom civil war at Stamford Bridge is officially over. Clearlake Capital, led by Behdad Eghbali and Jose E. Feliciano, has acquired the remaining 38.4% of the club from Todd Boehly, Mark Walter, and Hansjorg Wyss. This ends a tense standoff that began when a major rift divided the ownership group earlier this year.

🏟️ Reports indicate the relationship fractured over crucial long-term decisions, most notably whether to redevelop Stamford Bridge or construct a brand-new stadium elsewhere. While both sides initially explored buying each other out, Clearlake's financial muscle ultimately secured them 100% control, forcing Boehly to step down from his role as chairman.

📈 Although the club claims day-to-day operations and transfer strategies won't change immediately, consolidating power under a single entity removes massive internal friction. Clearlake can now execute their long-term vision for the squad and infrastructure without boardroom compromise.
