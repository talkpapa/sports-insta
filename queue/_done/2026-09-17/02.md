🇪🇸 Spain have locked down their World Cup-winning manager Luis de la Fuente with a historic contract extension until 2032.

🏆 Since taking over from Luis Enrique in 2022, De la Fuente has transformed Spain into a dominant global force. He has won the Euros, the Nations League, and the World Cup, losing just twice in 41 competitive matches during his tenure.

💰 The massive extension is as much about financial reward as it is about long-term planning. RFEF president Rafael Louzan admitted that De la Fuente’s previous contract did not place him among the top ten best-paid international managers. This new deal elevates his salary to the elite bracket.

🌍 Crucially, this seven-year commitment ensures stability as Spain prepares to co-host and defend their World Cup title on home soil in 2030. Having been with the federation for 14 years across various youth levels, the manager is now set to finish his career at the very top.
