Former Scotland boss Steve Clarke has revealed why he forced the SFA into a contract he immediately resigned from.

⚽ Before the World Cup, Clarke felt the Scottish FA was deliberately stalling on contract negotiations to see how the tournament would go. To break the deadlock, he used the media to apply pressure and threatened to announce his departure before boarding the plane if a deal was not signed.

📝 The tactic worked, and the panic-stricken SFA handed him a new four-year contract. However, Clarke reveals he always possessed a secret plan: if Scotland failed to progress past the group stage for the first time, he would resign anyway regardless of the security his new deal offered.

🗣️ Following their elimination after losses to Morocco and Brazil, Clarke kept his word and stepped down. He spoke to senior players and concluded that the squad needed a fresh tactical perspective to successfully qualify for and compete in Euro 2028 under new head coach Sebastien Pocognoli.
