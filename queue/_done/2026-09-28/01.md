🔵 Chelsea have found their steel again. Here is how their summer business completely transformed their tactical setup.

🏃‍♀️ After losing their WSL crown last season, doubts crept in. But a crucial 1-0 win over rivals Arsenal, securing a third consecutive clean sheet and extending an unbeaten domestic run to 12 games, proves they are back to their best under Sonia Bompastor.

🎯 The key lies in strategic recruitment. French striker Melvine Malard, signed from Manchester United, has instantly upgraded their out-of-possession game. Her aggressive high press forces turnovers and allows Chelsea to control matches even without the ball.

🔄 This frontline energy has unlocked midfielder Keira Walsh. Shielded by 20-year-old Lexi Potter, Walsh now plays with total freedom, driving forward to create goals—including the strike that set up Alyssa Thompson’s decisive winner.

Can this revamped Chelsea side win back the WSL title this season?
