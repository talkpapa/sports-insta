⚽ Tottenham paid a League of Ireland record fee for 18-year-old Mason Melia before sending him on loan to Lincoln City.

📌 Despite his young age, Melia already accumulated nearly 100 first-team appearances for St Patrick’s Athletic. That rare level of senior experience at 18 sparked intense interest from clubs across Europe before Spurs finalized the record purchase.

📌 His ability to operate both as a focal point in attack and drop deep to build plays fits Tottenham’s long-term profile. However, rather than stalling in academy football, he was sent to Lincoln to secure immediate lower-league minutes.

📌 Lincoln struggled for central goal threat early in the season, making Melia an instant solution to their attacking shortage while giving him regular exposure to physical English defenders.

Will Melia prove ready for Tottenham's first team after his EFL loan spell?
