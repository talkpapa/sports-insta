Florian Wirtz’s £116m arrival at Liverpool was meant to transform the attack, but his transition has faced clear hurdles.

📊 While Wirtz continues to excel in European competition, the sheer physical intensity of the Premier League has limited his influence. Domestic opponents press him aggressively, causing his passing accuracy to drop significantly compared to his Champions League outings.

🔄 Tactical instability has also played a part. Initial plans under Arne Slot failed to lock down his best position, and while new head coach Andoni Iraola has deployed him as a true No 10, injuries to key attacking partners have hampered cohesion.

👀 To get the best out of their record signing, Liverpool may need to secure a dedicated defensive midfielder to offer Wirtz structural protection and tactical freedom.

Can Iraola build the right system to fully unlock Wirtz in England?
