📦 Why are Premier League giants sending their most expensive teenagers to battle it out in League One and League Two?

While under-21 leagues offer elite technical training, they cannot replicate the raw physicality and high stakes of professional lower leagues. Top-tier clubs are increasingly using the EFL as a vital proving ground for their best prospects.

From City's teenage goalkeeper Whatmuff at Stockport to Tottenham's record-signing forward Mason Melia at Lincoln, these players are being pushed out of their comfort zones. They are sent to learn from veteran defenders who have spent decades mastering the dark arts of the game.
