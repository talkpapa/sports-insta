MLS clubs just smashed their summer transfer spending record by 65%, but the secret behind the boom isn't just wealthy owners.

💸 Major League Soccer has historically been constrained by complex salary restrictions and internal currency. However, the introduction of a rule allowing clubs to use traditional cash transfer fees instead of General Allocation Money (GAM) has completely transformed how teams operate in the market.

🔄 This new cash-for-player system does not replace GAM, but actually supercharges it. Clubs are allowed to convert up to $3 million in external transfer revenues back into GAM annually, providing them with much more financial flexibility to buy down roster cap hits.

📈 Backed by nearly $800 million in outbound player sales since 2022 and the upcoming 2026 World Cup, American clubs are aggressively reinvesting. This dual-currency loop has made MLS rosters younger, deeper, and far more expensive than ever before.
