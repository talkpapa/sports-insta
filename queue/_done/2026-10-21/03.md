Sarina Wiegman has extended her England contract until 2029, but her biggest tactical rebuild is just beginning.

📝 The FA locked down their back-to-back Euro winner to lead the national team through a critical transition period. While Wiegman brought unprecedented glory, recent play-off battles and a heavy 4-0 defeat to world champions Spain highlighted that the current squad model requires an immediate overhaul.

⌛ The core players who defined England's golden era are entering the final stages of their careers. Stalwarts like Lucy Bronze and Alex Greenwood will be late in their 30s by Euro 2029, forcing Wiegman to find dependable replacements despite a shrinking pool of homegrown starters in the WSL.

🔄 Debutants like Chelsea's Lexi Potter offer promise, but converting prospects into proven international starters remains the primary objective. Wiegman has handed out 24 debuts during her tenure, yet very few have secured permanent starting roles.

Can Wiegman successfully rebuild the Lionesses in time for the next tournament cycle?
