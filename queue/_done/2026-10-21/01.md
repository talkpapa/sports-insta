Pedro Neto's new Chelsea deal sends a clear message about how the club intends to run its long-term contract policy. 📝 Chelsea have locked down Pedro Neto on an improved five-year deal with a club option for an extra year. The Portuguese winger rejected summer approaches from Premier League rivals, including Manchester City, choosing to stay under head coach Xabi Alonso. 💼 While Chelsea's famous long-term contracts are designed to secure value, the hierarchy is showing a willingness to raise wages for top performers. This approach heavily contrasts with Enzo Fernández, who was sold to City for £125m after submitting a transfer request and severing team ties. 📊 By rewarding players like Neto, James, and Caicedo, Stamford Bridge bosses want to prove that long contracts won't trap players on fixed earnings if they perform and stay loyal. Can Chelsea's contract model balance financial control with player happiness long term?
