❌ Crystal Palace have shut down Manchester City's inquiry for rising England star Adam Wharton.

‒ Selhurst Park is standing firm. With three years remaining on Wharton's contract, the club holds absolute leverage and has made it clear he is not for sale. Manager Pierre Sage insists that after losing Maxence Lacroix, now is not the moment to sell key squad assets.

‒ To patch their defence, Palace have agreed a loan deal for Chelsea's Axel Disasi after failing to sign teammate Josh Acheampong. Disasi is set for his medical on Friday, providing immediate Premier League experience to Sage's backline.

‒ More outgoings could follow to balance the books. While a twenty-six million pound bid from Galatasaray for Ismaïla Sarr was rejected, right-back Daniel Muñoz is valued at twenty-five million pounds and could leave due to Palace's newly signed right-sided cover.

Can Palace successfully protect their core stars before the window closes?
