💸 Manchester United's debt has climbed past £1.1bn, revealing the true cost of their summer rebuild.

Financial filings show the club spent £191.7m on new players this summer, significantly more than the £153m initially reported for their main signings. To cover the shortfall and maintain cash flow, United drew down £120m from credit facilities between July and August, later repaying £30m.

This highlights how elite clubs use debt to fund transfers. United still owe £375m in outstanding transfer fees to other teams, with the majority of these payments due within the next 12 months. While Sir Jim Ratcliffe's cost-cutting measures are underway, the historic debt of £578m combined with active credit lines and transfer obligations keeps the financial pressure high.
