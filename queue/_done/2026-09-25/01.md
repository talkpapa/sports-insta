🇮🇹 How did Roma manage to sign Porto's highly-rated €70m playmaker Mora for just €25m this summer?

📉 Porto were fully prepared to hold onto their 19-year-old starlet until his €70m release clause was triggered in 2025. However, a sudden shift under new manager Francesco Farioli saw the playmaker fall out of favour, forcing a rapid re-evaluation of his future.

💼 Roma capitalised on the friction by offering a lower €25m upfront fee. To convince Porto to sell, the Italians structured the agreement with a hefty 50% sell-on fee, which Roma can choose to buy out in the future to keep full ownership.

⚽ The move has already paid off, with Mora eager to play under the management and scoring his first goal against Lecce. It represents one of the most calculated, high-value negotiations of the summer window.
