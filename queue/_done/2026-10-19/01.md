The Premier League signed 100 teenagers in five years, creating an internal market where top talent stays in England. ⚽ Massive spending power allows Premier League clubs to pay high fees for raw teenagers, such as Newcastle paying £20m for an 18-year-old with only 20 senior games. Over five seasons, English sides completed 100 permanent teen signings compared to just 29 in La Liga. 📊 This aggressive recruitment alters global transfer flows. Because top prospects move to England so early, mature world-class talent is concentrated within the league, forcing elite clubs to trade domestically. 💸 New financial and squad cost regulations further encourage high-volume player trading between Premier League rivals. Will European giants be completely priced out of future elite talent?
