Two major transfer collapses on deadline day have sparked a fierce debate over how clubs treat African players.

📢 Diomansy Kamara, agent for Senegal forward Ismaïla Sarr, has accused clubs of treating African talents like mere commodities. The statement followed a chaotic deadline day where Crystal Palace reportedly rejected Liverpool’s bid for Sarr, and Monaco pulled out of a £47m agreement with Chelsea for midfielder Lamine Camara.

🤝 Monaco defended their decision to cancel Camara's move, explaining that it was directly tied to another transfer. Once Everton resolved medical doubts and agreed to proceed with a £40m deal for forward Folarin Balogun, Monaco felt they no longer needed to sell Camara and pulled out of the Chelsea deal.

💔 The sudden U-turn left Chelsea furious and the players disappointed. Sarr reportedly deleted all references to Palace from his social media after his blocked move, while his agent warned that players' ambitions and voices are too often pushed into the background during high-stakes negotiations.
