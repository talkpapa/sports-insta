💰 Celtic have officially posted a £4.8m loss after tax for the year ending June 30, marking a sharp reversal from their £33.9m profit a year ago. 📉 The primary driver behind the 22.7% drop in overall revenue to £111m was missing out on Champions League group stage income. High squad turnover—including the exits of key stars like Daizen Maeda, Arne Engels, and Reo Hatate—plus costly managerial changes created additional instability, even though the club maintains a solid £66.4m cash reserve in the bank. ❓ How much will this loss restrict Celtic's budget in upcoming transfer windows?
