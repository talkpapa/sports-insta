🚨 Tottenham face a deadline decision as Richarlison closes in on a late departure to Turkish side Trabzonspor.

📋 The Brazilian forward repeatedly asked to leave North London this summer, according to manager Roberto De Zerbi. Spurs currently hold 18 senior non-homegrown players on their roster, exceeding the Premier League limit by one. Sanctioning Richarlison's exit allows the club to resolve their registration surplus while offloading a player who turned down an Everton return.

🇹🇷 Trabzonspor are eager for offensive firepower after a poor start to their domestic season. Personal terms are reportedly agreed with the 29-year-old striker, giving Tottenham a final opportunity to strike a deal before Turkey's transfer window shuts on Friday night.
