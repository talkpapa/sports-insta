Arsenal have secured their long-term future by agreeing a new contract with manager Mikel Arteta.

🤝 Reports indicate that a total agreement has been in place for a couple of weeks. While the club has not officially announced the extension, the final signing and formal announcement are expected to occur during the current international break.

🏆 Arteta's previous deal was set to expire next summer. After guiding the Gunners to their first Premier League title in 22 years last season and establishing them as consistent European contenders, extending his stay was the absolute priority for the board.

📈 By wrapping up negotiations quietly before the season intensifies, Arsenal avoid any unnecessary media speculation surrounding their manager's future. The Spanish tactician can now focus entirely on defending the league title and launching another deep Champions League run.
