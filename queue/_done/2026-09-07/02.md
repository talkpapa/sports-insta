🗳️ Gianni Infantino is refusing to back down, confirming he will stand for FIFA re-election despite massive backlash.

📉 The Swiss executive faces heavy pressure to resign from UEFA and Concacaf after attempting to sell stakes in FIFA tournaments, including the World Cup, to private investors. Opponents view this as a commercialisation too far, driving a wedge between Infantino and long-time allies.

🤝 Yet, the path to his re-election remains highly viable. To secure another term until 2031, he only requires a simple majority from the 211 member federations. Support from powerhouse blocs like South Americas Conmebol, Africas CAF, and 2034 hosts Saudi Arabia keeps him in a position of strength.

⚔️ A challenge is brewing, however, with Concacaf president Victor Montagliani widely tipped to run against him. With candidate nominations closing on November 18, the battle for the future control of global footballs commercial assets is officially on.
