Bradley Barcola has completed a stunning £123m move to Liverpool, fulfilling a childhood dream in the process.

🔴 The French winger has officially traded Paris for Merseyside. While Barcola previously dreamed of playing at Anfield as a teenager, the opportunity arose after his game time at PSG was severely impacted by squad competition.

🇫🇷 Following the January 2025 arrival of Khvicha Kvaratskhelia, Barcola was relegated to the bench for major matches. Because he is far less effective on the right wing or through the center, a transfer became the most logical move for his development.

💰 The financial details of the deal are historic. PSG secured a profit of nearly €100m on the player they originally bought from Lyon for €45m, helping the French champions register a record-breaking summer window for player sales.
