🚨 Arsenal have sanctioned their biggest sale in history as Gabriel Martinelli joins Al-Hilal for £60m.

💰 After falling down the pecking order at the Emirates, the 25-year-old Brazilian leaves North London in a milestone deal that eclipses the £40m fee Arsenal received for Alex Oxlade-Chamberlain in 2017. The agreement is understood to include a future sell-on clause, giving Arsenal potential upside down the line.

🇸🇦 For Martinelli, the move offers a lucrative new chapter, with reports indicating a four-year contract worth around £17m per season. His departure comes alongside outgoing moves for Gabriel Jesus and Fábio Vieira as Arsenal reshape their squad balance.
