📈 MLS clubs blew past previous spending records this summer, dropping nearly $190M on transfers in a 65% year-over-year surge.

💰 Major League Soccer surpassed $410M in total transfer spending for the year, more than double its 2023 total. The market growth comes as clubs prepare for potential roster rule overhauls and the approaching 2026 World Cup.

⚙️ The primary engine behind this boom is a rule change allowing direct cash-for-player trades between MLS clubs rather than relying solely on General Allocation Money. Teams can convert up to $3M in eligible transfer proceeds back into allocation money annually, giving front offices massive cap flexibility to build deeper rosters.

💸 Outbound sales are also supplying unprecedented liquidity, with MLS generating nearly $800M from player exports since 2022. Recent moves include teenager Lucas Herrington joining Hull City for $17M and Zavier Gozo moving to Crystal Palace for $15M.

Can MLS evolve into one of the world's top talent-exporting leagues?
