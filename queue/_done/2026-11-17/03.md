Crystal Palace hired Thomas Frank on a two-and-a-half-year contract after promising him funds to overhaul the squad in January.

🚨 Palace moved quickly for their primary target after sacking Pierre Sage, who leaves with a compensation bill reported closer to £6m. Frank turned down Palace in the summer to take a break from management, but Steve Parish led fresh negotiations to seal the deal amid rival interest from Newcastle and Fulham.

📊 The key priority for Frank is fixing a defensive collapse. Palace sit bottom of the division having conceded 24 goals in 10 league matches. Sage fell out with the board after 11 summer arrivals failed to fix squad balance, made worse when defender Joel Ordóñez failed his medical due to a foot fracture.

🛠️ Backroom preparations are already underway, with Justin Cochrane expected to join from the England setup alongside talks for Chelsea set-piece specialist Bernardo Cueva. The club has promised Frank immediate backing when the winter transfer window opens.
