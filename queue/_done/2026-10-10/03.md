How a single medical dispute cost Everton a £40m striker and blocked Chelsea's £47m signing.

🤝 Monaco CEO Thiago Scuro has revealed the wild sequence of events that left three clubs empty-handed on deadline day. Everton had agreed a £40m fee for striker Folarin Balogun but paused to renegotiate after raising late medical concerns, which quickly leaked to the press.

🔄 To cover their financial needs, Monaco quickly agreed a separate £47m deal to sell midfielder Lamine Camara to Chelsea. However, when Everton suddenly revived the Balogun deal at 10 PM, Monaco pulled out of the Chelsea agreement, preferring to keep Camara and sell the striker.

❌ The gamble backfired spectacularly. Despite both clubs signing the official paperwork, Balogun refused to sign his contract at the final second, feeling insulted by how Everton’s medical department publicly cast doubt on his health.
