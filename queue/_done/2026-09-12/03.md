🇹🇿 Washington Spirit have completed a blockbuster move for Tanzanian striker Clara Luvanga from Saudi club Al-Nassr. While the official transfer fee remains undisclosed, reports suggest the American club paid up to $2.3 million to secure her signature. If verified, this figure would shatter the previous women's transfer record, though sources close to the deal already place it firmly in the top five most expensive signings in history. Spirit's sporting director James Hocken highlighted Luvanga's versatile goalscoring ability as a crucial asset for their upcoming NWSL play-off push.
