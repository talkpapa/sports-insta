🚨 Arsenal finished the transfer window without adding a marquee forward, leaving Mikel Arteta with a noticeably thinner front line for the season.

⚽ The Gunners reshaped their squad early by bringing in quality additions like Bruno Guimarães and Ezri Konsa. However, their primary offensive target Morgan Rogers opted for Chelsea, forcing Arsenal to pivot toward marquee names like Real Madrid's Vinícius Júnior and Atlético Madrid's Julián Alvarez.

💼 When Vinícius signed a contract extension in Spain and Alvarez rejected a late move, Arsenal's hierarchy decided against panic buying late in the window. Instead of spending on secondary options, they chose to keep their powder dry despite already sanctioning major outgoings.

📉 With Gabriel Jesus departing for Barcelona and Ethan Nwaneri heading to Borussia Dortmund, the lack of depth up front leaves Arteta taking a massive risk as domestic and European fixtures pile up.
