Richarlison could leave Tottenham for Trabzonspor before Friday's deadline as Spurs look to solve a squad bottleneck.

📌 Manager Roberto De Zerbi confirmed the striker requested a move multiple times this summer after a potential return to Everton fell through. The 29-year-old Brazilian has now reportedly agreed personal terms with Trabzonspor ahead of the Turkish deadline.

💰 Tottenham have clear registration reasons to complete a deal: they carry 18 senior non-homegrown players, which is one over the Premier League limit. Moving Richarlison—who enters his final contract year—frees up vital squad space without requiring extra squad omissions.

🇹🇷 Trabzonspor are pushing for firepower following a disappointing start to their domestic campaign and early European elimination.
