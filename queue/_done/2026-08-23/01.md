💸 Spending £300m in a single transfer window doesn't guarantee instant success on the pitch.

⚽ Tottenham's new post-Levy era began with a massive spending spree, including the £100m signing of Sandro Tonali and £85m on Mateus Fernandes. However, Roberto De Zerbi’s side looked completely uncoordinated in their 3-0 opening day defeat to Brentford, fielding four debutants who struggled to find any tactical rhythm.

🎯 In contrast, Brentford demonstrated the power of smart recruitment and tactical continuity. Their £39m midfield signing Mamadou Sangaré from Lens dominated the pitch, proving far more effective than Spurs' expensive midfield because he was integrated into an already established, highly cohesive system.

📈 This result highlights a key transfer market reality: buying individuals is only half the battle. Without tactical drilling and time to gel, even a £300m squad overhaul can look disjointed against a well-run, stable opponent.

Can De Zerbi mold this expensive group of individuals into a cohesive team quickly?
