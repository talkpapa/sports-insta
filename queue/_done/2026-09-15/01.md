Borussia Mönchengladbach have sacked Eugen Polanski after just three games, exposing years of disastrous squad management.

📉 Eugen Polanski's exit after a 5-0 thrashing by Freiburg marks Gladbach's second consecutive season sacking a manager just three games in. The former academy graduate stepped up from the Under-23s to save the club last year, but he was set up to fail with a critically depleted roster.

💸 The root cause is a catastrophic failure in transfer strategy. Once a Champions League knockout side boasting Yann Sommer, Matthias Ginter, and Marcus Thuram, Gladbach repeatedly allowed premium talent to leave on free transfers instead of generating vital reinvestment funds.

🔄 New sporting director Rouven Schröder oversaw a massive summer overhaul to fix the squad, but the new signings have failed to gel. Internal tension is already boiling over, highlighted by captain Tim Kleindienst publicly criticizing teammates' focus before his costly red card.

Can Gladbach avoid the drop with this squad?
