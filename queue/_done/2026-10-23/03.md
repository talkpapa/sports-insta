⚽ A major shift is on the horizon for one of English football's most unique clubs. Ryan Bertrand is leading a group aiming to inject £87m into AFC Wimbledon, but the structure of this deal is designed specifically to guard the club's fan-owned heritage.

The investment vehicle, Sloane Street Capital, would provide an initial £16m for a 34% share, with further funds distributed over five years. The Dons Trust retains majority voting rights and has built-in veto powers over the stadium freehold and the club's core identity markers like the badge and kit colours.

Legal checks on funding sources are already complete, and members are set to vote on the proposal this November. This is a rare example of private capital entering the EFL while intentionally leaving the keys to the club’s history in the hands of the supporters.
