Arsenal prioritized elite quality over emergency bodies, but did it leave Mikel Arteta's squad dangerously thin?

🚨 The champions experienced a surprisingly quiet deadline day, allowing Gabriel Jesus to leave and selling Gabriel Martinelli to Al-Hilal for a record £60m. Despite these major departures, no new attacking reinforcements arrived to share the goalscoring burden.

💼 Sporting director Andrea Berta focused the club's summer efforts on dream signings like Vinícius Júnior and Julián Alvarez, but Alvarez preferred Barcelona and Juventus refused to sell Kenan Yildiz. Under Arteta's instruction, Arsenal also rejected a move for Malick Fofana due to doubts over whether he would genuinely improve the starting XI.

📈 While additions like Bruno Guimarães and Ezri Konsa provide elite cover elsewhere, the decision to balance the net spend down to £78m leaves Bukayo Saka highly exposed. Arsenal are betting heavily on squad continuity, but a single injury upfront could derail their entire title defense.

Can Arsenal retain their title without replacing their departed forwards?
