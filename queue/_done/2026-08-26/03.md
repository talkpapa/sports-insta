🔴 Manchester United have completed a £70m deal for Carlos Baleba, taking their midfield spending to nearly £150m this summer.

The deal is structured as £65m guaranteed plus £5m in add-ons, alongside a sell-on clause for Brighton. The 22-year-old Cameroon international has committed to a five-year contract with a club option for an additional year, following United's failed attempt to sign him last summer.

The transfer became a priority after a sudden crisis in the engine room. With Casemiro departing and new signing Manuel Ugarte suffering a serious knee injury, United had to act fast, adding Baleba to previous summer arrivals Andrey Santos and Youri Tielemans.

Baleba will work under Michael Carrick, who was a key factor in his decision. Despite an underwhelming campaign last season, United's recruitment team, led by Jason Wilcox, remained convinced of his elite potential in the Premier League.
