Nottingham Forest are breaking their transfer record with a £50m move for Chelsea striker Liam Delap.

🚨 Nottingham Forest have reached an agreement to sign 23-year-old striker Liam Delap for a club-record £50m. The forward is set for a medical this week after Chelsea decided to cash in on him just one year after signing him for £30m from Ipswich Town.

📈 Chelsea are happy to secure a £20m profit on a player who struggled with shoulder and hamstring issues, scoring just one Premier League goal last term. With new attacking arrivals Emmanuel Emegha and Danny Welbeck already through the door at Stamford Bridge, Delap became surplus to requirements.

🎯 For Forest, the move is a direct response to their toothless opening-day 1-0 defeat to Leeds, where Chris Wood and Igor Jesus failed to impress. Having already spent £34m on defender Ousmane Diomande, owner Evangelos Marinakis is backing the manager with another heavy investment to solve their goalscoring woes.
