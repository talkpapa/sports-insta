🔴 Manchester United have beaten fierce competition to land Swedish winger Monica Jusu Bah in a major £400k deal.

🇸🇪 The 23-year-old Sweden international arrives from BK Hacken on a four-year contract, becoming United's third signing of the window. Having featured heavily in Sweden's recent World Cup qualifying matches, Jusu Bah brings proven international quality to help bolster United's attacking options for the upcoming WSL season.

💰 Because she was tied to BK Hacken until 2028, United had to shell out just under £400,000 to break the contract. Club sources confirm that moving swiftly was essential, as multiple European rivals were hovering to secure her signature before the transfer window slams shut.

🤝 The winger highlighted the chance to work under manager Eva Olid as a key factor in her decision to move to Manchester. United are not done yet, with reports suggesting they hope to secure at least one more signing before the deadline.
