Scottish football has taken the first major step toward scrapping the traditional Saturday 3pm TV blackout.

📺 Under current broadcast deals, Sky Sports and Premier Sports show just 82 of the 228 top-flight Scottish Premiership matches each season—leaving 64% of games untelevised. The blackout rule, enforced via UEFA Article 48 when half of a country's top divisions play simultaneously, was originally designed to protect stadium attendances.

💰 With streaming now dominating global sports consumption, clubs argue the blackout severely restricts potential media income. The SPFL is structuring its 2029–30 broadcast tender with maximum flexibility, allowing TV networks to bid for full live coverage of all 228 matches, provided they do not enforce the 3pm restriction.

🗳️ Any final proposal will require approval from at least eight of the 12 Scottish Premiership clubs. Because rights deals in Scotland, England, and Wales all end in 2028–29, Scotland's move could create a blueprint for the rest of British football.
