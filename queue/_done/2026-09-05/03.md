Coventry City have completed a £10.3m deal for Toulouse winger Yann Gboho after receiving essential FIFA ratification.

📝 The deadline day deal was submitted to the Premier League prior to the cut-off, but international transfers completed right at the deadline require secondary approval from FIFA. With clearance now granted, the 25-year-old winger only needs UK visa approval before joining Frank Lampard's squad.

💰 The move pushes promoted Coventry's summer outlay past the £100m mark, making Gboho their twelfth new arrival of the window. The former Rennes and Cercle Brugge attacker leaves Toulouse after scoring 19 goals in 83 appearances, bringing needed width to a side that lost its opening two Premier League fixtures.
