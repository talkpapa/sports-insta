James Trafford started both cup finals for Man City last season, yet still pushed for a summer transfer to Leeds.

🧤 James Trafford returned to Manchester City in July 2025, but his situation changed rapidly when Gianluigi Donnarumma arrived from PSG weeks later. The Italian instantly took the starting spot, leaving Trafford with only one Premier League start across the final 35 league matches.

🏆 Despite starting both domestic cup finals at Wembley and lifting two trophies with City, Trafford admitted that sitting on the bench took a heavy toll on his mental wellbeing. He realized that remaining on the sidelines would stall his career development and international ambitions.

📋 Now at Leeds United, the 24-year-old goalkeeper is securing regular starting minutes. He aims to establish himself as England's primary goalkeeper by proving his quality every week in the Premier League.

Can Trafford earn the England starting spot through his performances at Leeds?
