Liverpool spent big on Isak, Wirtz, and Barcola, but deliberately left key positions uncovered this summer. Here is why.

📌 Despite boasting the Premier League’s highest net spend after landing Bradley Barcola for up to £123m alongside Alexander Isak and Florian Wirtz, Liverpool opted against signing a natural right-winger, right-back, or defensive midfielder before the deadline.

🧠 Head coach Andoni Iraola backed the board’s refusal to throw crazy money at short-term squad fillers while Hugo Ekitiké, Conor Bradley, and Giovanni Leoni recover from injuries. Instead of overpaying for temporary options like Yankuba Minteh or Ismaïla Sarr, Liverpool preserved their financial flexibility.

⚽ Iraola plans to utilize flexible wingers—including Barcola, Cody Gakpo, Victor Muñoz, and Rio Ngumoha—across both flanks, trusting long-term planning over immediate market reaction.

Did Liverpool make the right call by waiting, or will squad depth cost them?
