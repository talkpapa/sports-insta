Tottenham beat European rivals to sign Mason Melia for a record fee, but immediately sent him on loan to Lincoln City. ⚽ Tottenham paid a record League of Ireland fee to secure the 18-year-old forward from St Patrick’s Athletic, where he had already accumulated nearly 100 senior appearances. Despite heavy interest from clubs across England and Europe, Spurs won the race for his signature in January. 📈 Rather than keeping him in youth football, Spurs chose a League One loan to accelerate his growth. Melia offers versatile attacking qualities, able to lead the line or drop deep into midfield to link play. 🎯 Lincoln City provides an immediate target for his development, having recently recorded just one shot on goal from 18 attempts. The move gives Melia key first-team minutes in a demanding physical league. Will Melia break into Tottenham’s first team after his League One loan?
