Manchester City shelled out £116m for Elliot Anderson because he offered something no other available midfielder could replicate.

🧠 When rival clubs were scouting alternative options, Enzo Maresca pushed to focus on Anderson. The manager identified him as a future cornerstone for club and country, prompting City to win the race against interest from Manchester United and Arsenal.

📊 The massive fee reflected his extraordinary underlying numbers at Nottingham Forest, where he topped the Premier League in touches, duels won, and ball recoveries despite playing in a struggling side. That rare work-rate allowed him to step straight into the tactical role previously split between Rodri and Bernardo Silva.

⚽ Operating as a deep-lying number six, Anderson has maintained elite progression metrics, trailing only a handful of players in line-breaking passes while winning 66 duels and drawing 28 fouls already this season.

Is £116m looking like smart business for City?
