⚽ Premier League teams are fundamentally reshaping how the global transfer market operates by buying unproven teenagers for massive fees.

📊 This summer saw massive deals for 18-year-olds: Ayyoub Bouaddi joined Manchester City for £81.5m, Aston Villa paid £47m for Ibrahim Mbaye after fewer than 1,000 Ligue 1 minutes, and Newcastle spent £20m on Sean Steur after just 20 senior appearances. Over the last five seasons, English clubs have signed 100 teenagers—compared to just 29 in La Liga and 47 in Ligue 1.

💸 By vacuuming up global talent so early, Premier League sides create an insular market. Once these young players develop, rival European leagues are priced out, leaving English clubs to trade mature stars almost exclusively amongst each other to satisfy squad cost rules.
