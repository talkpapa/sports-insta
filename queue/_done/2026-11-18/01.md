⚠️ The business of football is facing new scrutiny regarding how player agency is balanced against club authority.

Recent deadline day sagas involving African stars have sparked a debate on whether players are being treated as assets rather than individuals. Agents are highlighting that while clubs hold the final legal say, the lack of consideration for a player's career trajectory and personal ambitions creates a perception that talent is merely a tool for accounting.

Clubs often view these decisions as 'just business'—using one transfer to facilitate another or pulling out of deals due to medical uncertainty elsewhere. However, the human cost remains high when a player's future is left in limbo. Do you think players need more legal protection in these high-stakes negotiations?
