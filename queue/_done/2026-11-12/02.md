Premier League clubs signed 100 teenagers over five years compared to just 29 in La Liga.

📊 English sides are buying raw talent before they even establish themselves elsewhere. Newcastle paid £20m for 18-year-old Sean Steur after just 20 senior Ajax games, while Manchester City spent £81.5m on 18-year-old Ayyoub Bouaddi and Aston Villa committed £47m for PSG teenager Ibrahim Mbaye.

⚙️ This massive spending power concentrates the global talent pool inside England. Combined with squad cost ratio rules that encourage active player movement, Premier League clubs increasingly trade exclusively among themselves because rival European teams are priced out.
