Why Arsenal chose to walk away from deadline day panic despite losing key forward options.

⚽ Mikel Arteta wanted deeper squad options for a four-competition campaign, but Arsenal allowed Gabriel Jesus and Gabriel Martinelli to exit without adding late attacking reinforcements.

💶 The club's transfer policy under sporting director Andrea Berta focused on top-tier targets like Vinícius Júnior and Julián Álvarez. When those deals proved unavailable, Arsenal refused to panic buy, turning down moves for lower-tier options like Malick Fofana to protect squad standards.

📊 Martinelli's £60m move to Al-Hilal brought Arsenal's summer net spend down to £78m, following earlier deals for Eberechi Eze, Bruno Guimarães, and Ezri Konsa. The champions chose internal continuity over compromising on player quality.
