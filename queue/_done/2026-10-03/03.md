🚨 Transfer dominoes explained: Why Monaco pulled the plug on Chelsea's £47m deal at the very last minute.

📦 Monaco had a written agreement with Chelsea for Lamine Camara. However, when Everton resolved issues surrounding Folarin Balogun’s medical and agreed to complete a £40m transfer, Monaco immediately cancelled Camara’s move. With Balogun's departure finalised, Monaco chose to retain Camara.

❌ Meanwhile, Crystal Palace blocked Ismaïla Sarr’s proposed move to Liverpool. Sarr was reportedly left deeply disappointed, even deleting Palace references from his social media. Liverpool eventually dropped their pursuit after deciding to keep Cody Gakpo on deadline day.

🗣️ These late collapses prompted agent Diomansy Kamara to speak out. He argued that African players are too often treated as mere commodities in negotiations, with clubs dictating terms while ignoring the players' personal ambitions and career choices.

Should players have more power to force through these deadline day moves?
