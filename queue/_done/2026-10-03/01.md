🇮🇹 Roma secured one of Europe's biggest bargains by exploiting a manager feud and inserting a clever buyout clause.

Porto fully expected European giants to trigger 19-year-old playmaker Mora’s €70m (£59.5m) release clause by 2025. However, after the teenager fell out of favour under new head coach Francesco Farioli, his valuation plummeted. Roma capitalised on the tension immediately.

Instead of paying the massive clause, Roma negotiated a cut-price €25m (£21.4m) deal. To convince Porto, they included a huge 50% sell-on fee. Crucially, Roma negotiated a clause allowing them to buy out this sell-on percentage in the future, protecting his long-term value.

Did Roma pull off the transfer window's smartest piece of business?
