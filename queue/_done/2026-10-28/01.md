Barcelona received a tiny €602 fine after Atletico Madrid reported an improper transfer approach for Julian Alvarez.

🚨 Atletico Madrid officially complained after Barcelona contacted striker Julian Alvarez without providing required written notification first. Despite reporting the issue to both governing bodies, FIFA rejected jurisdiction over the domestic case, leaving the Spanish FA to issue a strictly nominal statutory fine.

💰 Alvarez remains committed to Atletico under his long-term contract running until June 2030, following his £81.5m transfer from Manchester City in August 2024. The striker recently publicly apologized to Atletico supporters for expressing a desire to join Barcelona during summer interviews.

📊 With 51 goals in 115 matches for Atletico, Alvarez scored a crucial winning goal against Deportivo La Coruna to close the title race gap to just two points behind Barcelona.
