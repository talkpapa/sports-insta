Liverpool have completed a dramatic £123m deal for PSG forward Bradley Barcola after months of internal squad shifts in Paris.

🇫🇷 The French international prioritized an exit after being dropped to the bench in major European fixtures. Following the arrival of Khvicha Kvaratskhelia at PSG, Barcola found his preferred left-wing starting role limited, despite impressing at the recent World Cup.

💰 For PSG, selling Barcola unlocked a record-breaking financial windfall. Having bought him from Lyon for €45m, the French champions generated nearly €100m in pure profit, headlining a window where they collected roughly €350m in sales.

🔴 Former youth coaches revealed that joining Liverpool was a childhood dream for Barcola, making negotiations swift once Anfield extended an offer.
