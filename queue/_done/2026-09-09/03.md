🚐 An 18-year-old goalkeeper drove 2,000 miles across Europe in a converted van just to find his next club.

📍 Leaving Northern Irish side Glentoran with no guaranteed contract, Milo Beimers and his father embarked on a month-long road trip across Spain and England. Living in a converted transit van, the teenager took trials where he could get them, refusing to give up on his professional dreams.

🇮🇹 A sudden call from Italy redirected their journey. Beimers impressed on trial at Viterbese and officially signed with the Serie D side. The club, merging under the Vigor Campagnano Gialloblu banner, has a 7,000-seat stadium and immediate ambitions to secure promotion back to Serie C.

🗣️ The Canada youth international is now adapting to a new country and a completely different tactical style of goalkeeping. Despite the language barrier, he has already kept a clean sheet on his debut and could eventually face Serie A giants in the Coppa Italia.
