Major League Soccer just smashed its summer spending record, shelling out nearly $190 million in a single window.

📈 The massive 65% year-over-year spike in summer expenditure is driven by a critical regulatory shift. MLS clubs can now trade players using traditional cash rather than just General Allocation Money (GAM), unlocking new flexibility in squad building.

💰 This rule allows clubs to convert up to $3 million of their transfer profits back into GAM annually. As a result, more money than ever is circulating through the league to help teams buy down salary cap hits and acquire higher-profile targets.

🎓 This spending is heavily subsidized by an increasingly lucrative academy pipeline. MLS clubs have secured nearly $800 million from outbound transfers since 2022, selling teenage prospects to top-tier European leagues for multi-million dollar fees.
