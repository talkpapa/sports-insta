Newly promoted Premier League clubs just spent a record-breaking sum exceeding £470m in a single transfer window.

💰 The traditional strategy of promoted sides keeping their Championship core is officially dead. Ipswich Town led the charge by spending £194.7m on 15 new additions, while Hull City brought in 18 players for £156.5m and Coventry spent £122.4m on 11 recruits.

📈 Back in 2021, all three promoted teams combined spent under £99m. Today's massive influx reflects how newcomer clubs feel forced to reshape entire squads immediately to survive against established Premier League giants.

📊 This extreme spending highlights the wider economic dominance of the Premier League, where eight clubs recorded net spends over £100m this window alone.

Can newly promoted teams spend their way into Premier League permanence?
