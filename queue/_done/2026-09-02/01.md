🚨 Lamine Camara's proposed £47.1m switch to Chelsea is off after failing to beat the transfer deadline.

💼 The 22-year-old Senegal international completed his medical, but the deal collapsed shortly after the 23:00 BST cutoff. Monaco had lowered their asking price late in the window when Folarin Balogun's move to Everton stalled, opening the door for Chelsea to strike a deal.

💰 Chelsea wanted Camara to offset the loss of Enzo Fernandez, who is nearing a £125m move to Manchester City. Despite missing out on Camara, Chelsea feel they strengthened their squad this summer with the additions of Morgan Rogers, Maxence Lacroix, and Emiliano Martinez.
