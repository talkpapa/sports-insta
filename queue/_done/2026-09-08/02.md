⚽️ Monaco are top of Ligue 1 with a perfect record, but their failed deadline day sales have sparked a quiet financial crisis. 🇫🇷 Monaco had a clear plan to satisfy the DNCG, French football's financial watchdog. They aimed to sell star striker Folarin Balogun to Everton and midfielder Lamine Camara to Chelsea to slash their wage bill and generate major capital. ❌ Everything collapsed when Balogun refused the Everton move despite club agreement. Because he stayed, Monaco could not free up a non-EU squad spot to register a replacement midfielder, forcing them to pull out of Camara's agreed sale to Chelsea. 💰 While manager Filipe Luis now enjoys a squad featuring both stars, the club's executives are left scrambling to find alternative solutions to balance the books. Can Monaco balance their books without sacrificing their title charge in January?
