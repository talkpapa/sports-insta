📺 Valdas Dambrauskas won £725 on a TV game show and turned it into a Champions League coaching career.

💰 In 2002, the Lithuanian won 4,000 litas (about £725) on Lithuania's version of "Who Wants to Be a Millionaire?". Though a modest sum, it represented six months of average wages at home. He used every penny to relocate to London on a student visa with limited English, aiming to earn his coaching badges from scratch.

🛠️ What followed was eight years of intense survival. Dambrauskas worked as a gardener, handyman, and pub floor polisher to support his family while studying sports science. He volunteered for community projects in Finsbury Park, eventually becoming the first Lithuanian to earn a UEFA licence through the London FA.

🏟️ His persistence paid off with youth coaching roles at Manchester United Soccer Schools, Fulham, and Brentford. Now, at 49, he manages Azerbaijani side Sabah in the Champions League, returning to face the English giants where his dream first began.
