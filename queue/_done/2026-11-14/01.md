Pedro Neto has signed an improved five-year Chelsea contract after snubbing summer interest from Manchester City.

⚡ Pedro Neto rejected summer offers from rival clubs to remain at Stamford Bridge under head coach Xabi Alonso. Chelsea have now rewarded his commitment and strong performances as an attacking wingback with a five-year contract that includes an option for an extra season alongside a salary bump.

💰 The decision highlights Chelsea's contract philosophy under the current hierarchy. While the club binds talent to long-term deals, they actively use salary adjustments to incentivize players who perform and buy into the manager's tactical vision, following recent improved terms for players like Moisés Caicedo and Reece James.

🔄 Neto's commitment stands in sharp contrast to Enzo Fernández, who was sold to Manchester City for £125m late in the window after submitting a transfer request. The board decided to part ways with Fernández after concluding his relationship with teammates was broken, choosing instead to double down on committed core players.
