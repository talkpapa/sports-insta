Tottenham could still lose Richarlison this week as the Turkish transfer window remains open. 🇧🇷 The Brazilian forward has asked to leave Spurs multiple times this summer according to manager Roberto De Zerbi. After turning down an Everton return on Tuesday, he is now weighing up a move to Trabzonspor with personal terms reportedly agreed. 📊 Spurs currently have 18 senior non-homegrown players on their books, which is one over the Premier League squad limit of 17. Sanctioning Richarlison's exit before Friday's deadline allows Tottenham to trim their roster and solve their registration issue. 🇹🇷 Trabzonspor are pushing for the deal after a difficult start to their domestic and European campaigns, seeking to bolster their attack before their transfer window shuts on Friday night.
