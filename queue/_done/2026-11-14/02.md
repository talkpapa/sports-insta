🧩 Sporting director Julian Ward faces a strategic puzzle: extend their primary leader or transition to younger options like Jeremy Jacquet, alongside an option to buy Ronald Araujo. Losing Van Dijk removes irreplaceable leadership, but renewing him on current terms carries financial risk.
