Cole Palmer has extended his Chelsea contract until 2034 despite already having nine years remaining on his previous deal.

✍️ The England forward moved from Manchester City in 2023 and has already signed two contract adjustments since arriving. This latest extension adds another year to his package while providing a significant wage upgrade to match his standing as the squad's focal point.

🛡️ According to reports, this proactive move forms part of Chelsea's strategy to reward key figures early and ward off rival suitors. Core players like Moises Caicedo, Reece James, and Pedro Neto are similarly tied down long-term as the club locks in its core roster for their peak years.

📈 Despite recent on-pitch inconsistencies under Xabi Alonso, management is prioritizing stability behind the scenes to build a platform for future trophy contention.
