📈 Major League Soccer just shattered its summer spending record with nearly $190 million spent, pushing 2026 total layout past $410 million.

💰 The surge stems from revamped roster mechanics allowing direct cash-for-player trades. Crucially, clubs can now convert up to $3 million in transfer revenue into allocation money each year. Combined with nearly $800 million earned from selling young talent since 2022, teams hold unprecedented spending power.

🔮 With the 2026 World Cup approaching, this structural shift marks a turning point in how North American clubs build rosters. Will MLS soon compete with top European leagues for marquee signings?
