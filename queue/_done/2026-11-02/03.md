A £47m deadline day deal collapsed because of another transfer happening across the continent. 🚨 Chelsea thought they had secured midfielder Lamine Camara for £47m on deadline day, but Monaco pulled out of the agreement at the final hour. 💰 The sudden turnaround was triggered when Everton revived their £40m deal for Folarin Balogun. With Balogun's exit approved, Monaco no longer needed to sacrifice Camara to raise funds. 🗣️ The move sparked outrage from agent representatives over how players are treated when corporate priorities shift late in the window. Should clubs be allowed to back out of written agreements on deadline day?
