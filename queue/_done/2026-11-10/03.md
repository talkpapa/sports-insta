American investors have completed an 87.4% takeover of League Two club Shrewsbury Town, ending a 30-year era.

🇺🇸 Biscayne ST LLC, a US consortium headed by Scott Davidson, finalised the purchase of 87.4% of the club's shares. Former USA international goalkeeper Brad Friedel joins the project as the new sporting director to oversee squad strategy.

⌛ The deal brings an end to Roland Wycherley’s 30-year tenure. The 85-year-old had put the club up for sale more than five years ago, holding out for a buyer who could provide the financial backing required for future growth.

📈 With new board members awaiting final EFL approval, the club aims to utilize fresh capital to build momentum on and off the pitch.
