🚨 Newly promoted Premier League clubs spent over £470m combined this summer as Ipswich, Hull, and Coventry transformed squads.

💰 A few years ago, promoted teams took a cautious approach. In 2021, the three incoming sides spent just £98.8m combined. Fast forward to this window: Ipswich splashed £194.7m on 15 players, Hull added 18 signings for £156.5m, and Coventry spent £122.4m on 11 additions.

📊 The extreme financial gap between the Championship and top-flight football means surviving on promotion momentum is no longer viable. Teams must recruit immediately at Premier League scale or risk instant relegation.

Can these massive spending sprees guarantee Premier League survival?
