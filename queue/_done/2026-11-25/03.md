🤐 FA chair Debbie Hewitt had to sign an NDA just to view FIFA's hidden tournament sell-off files. Council members demanded answers after FIFA explored selling stakes in its competitions to private investors under the FIFA Forward Enterprise proposal.

📜 Instead of publishing full reports, FIFA forced executives to travel to Zurich to inspect selected files under heavy protocols. The gagging clause prevents member associations from speaking out or sharing findings publicly, preserving confidentiality amid ongoing legal action from UEFA.

⚖️ Critics argue the agreement dilutes accountability and protects executive power ahead of upcoming presidential elections. Should governing bodies be allowed to force member associations into signing gagging clauses?
