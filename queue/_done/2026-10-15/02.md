Rising stars like Yan Diomande saw their valuations soar after impressive displays for Côte d’Ivoire, leading to a massive nine-figure move to Real Madrid. The data shows that the tournament acts as a multiplier, turning potential into premium prices almost overnight.

While some elite clubs waited for the final whistle to bid, others like Tottenham moved aggressively during the group stages. It’s a complete shift in scouting strategy where performance in high-pressure matches dictates the final fee more than long-term league scouting alone.
