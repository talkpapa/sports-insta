🔴 Bradley Barcola’s £123m move to Liverpool fulfills a childhood dream, but the mechanics behind this mega-deal go far deeper.

Although Barcola was highly rated in Paris, the January 2025 arrival of Khvicha Kvaratskhelia severely limited his opportunities in major matches. Since Barcola struggles to replicate his left-wing threat on the right or through the middle, he found himself benched for key Champions League fixtures.

For PSG, the financial incentive was too big to ignore. The Ligue 1 side made a near €100m profit on the winger they bought for €45m from Lyon, helping them register a record-breaking €350m transfer window in player sales.
