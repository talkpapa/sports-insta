Folarin Balogun aborted a £40m move to Everton on deadline day after taking offence at how his medical was handled.

📝 The American international had traveled to Liverpool and signed a late deal sheet before the deadline. However, Everton's medical team raised last-minute fitness concerns and pushed to renegotiate the £40m fee structure with Monaco, while media reports claimed Balogun had failed his medical.

💼 Monaco chief executive Thiago Scuro revealed that Everton eventually accepted there was no physical issue and signed the final contracts. But Balogun personally refused to sign, feeling uncomfortable committing to a club that publicly doubted his health.

🔁 The collapse also ruined Chelsea's proposed £47m move for Monaco midfielder Lamine Camara. Monaco only entertained Chelsea's offer while Balogun was stalled, immediately pulling out once Everton agreed to proceed with Balogun.
