Brighton’s £12.5m deadline day move for Millwall’s Azeez is a classic data-driven market move targeting under-rewarded chance creation.📊 Reports confirm Brighton paid an initial £12.5m fee, potentially rising to £15m, to bring Azeez to the Premier League. The winger quietly dominated the second tier last term, ranking top for open-play crosses with 227 and producing 6.71 xA from crossing alone.🎯 His broader metrics tell an even clearer story of untapped potential. Azeez generated 9.12 total expected assists and created 18 big chances, but ended up with just seven assists due to Millwall’s finishing struggles.⚡ By placing him in a higher-tier setup with superior finishers, Brighton expect his underlying numbers to immediately translate into elite top-flight productivity.Will Azeez prove to be the bargain of deadline day?
