Critics scoffed at Enzo Fernandez's £125m price tag, but he just proved exactly why Enzo Maresca demanded him. 

⚽ When Manchester City paid Chelsea £125 million on deadline day, questions immediately arose about the midfielder's attitude and staggering valuation. But manager Enzo Maresca knew exactly what he was buying, having built a close bond with the Argentinian during their time together in London.

🏃‍♂️ The real test came during a fierce away derby when a first-half red card forced City to play with ten men. Fernandez instantly adapted, covering 10.25km of turf and executing twice as many sprints as any other central midfielder to carry out the defensive work of two players.

📊 Winning a team-high eight duels and recording the most interceptions, he completely disrupted the opponent's build-up play. This relentless energy and tactical flexibility are exactly why City bankrolled Maresca's post-Rodri midfield revolution.

Is Enzo Fernandez already proving he is worth the £125m fee?
