🚨 Jack Grealish's career stands at a critical juncture after being dropped by Everton for missing training ahead of their cup clash.

⚽ Jack Grealish rejoined Everton on loan from Manchester City late in the window, seeking regular football as he enters the final year of his City contract. However, his failure to report for training on Monday prompted manager David Moyes and club leadership to take a public stand by omitting him from the matchday squad.

📋 The move carries major stakes for both parties. Everton operates with a razor-thin squad of only 18 senior outfield players, making Grealish's creativity essential. Yet Moyes prioritized squad values, handing 20-year-old Tyrique George the start on the left wing in a 3-1 Carabao Cup victory over Newcastle.

💼 Following high-level internal discussions, Grealish is expected to be reintegrated into the team. But with his Manchester City era effectively over, the 31-year-old must avoid further indiscretions to safeguard his top-flight future.
