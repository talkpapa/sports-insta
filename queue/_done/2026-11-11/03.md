Arsenal preferred squad continuity over late panic buys after missing out on ambitious marquee targets on deadline day.

📌 While title rivals spent heavily, Arsenal allowed forward players Gabriel Jesus and Gabriel Martinelli to leave, bringing in £60m for Martinelli from Al-Hilal. Despite earlier additions like Bruno Guimarães, Ezri Konsa, and Christos Tzolis, no replacement attackers arrived in the final hours.

💰 Sporting director Andrea Berta focused efforts on elite targets such as Vinícius Júnior, opting against secondary squad options like Malick Fofana. This stance brought Arsenal's summer net spend down to £78m, significantly lower than their major rivals.

⚽ With attacking depth now thin behind key players like Bukayo Saka, Mikel Arteta is relying heavily on squad chemistry to defend the Premier League title.

Will Arsenal's decision to avoid panic buys cost them in the title race?
