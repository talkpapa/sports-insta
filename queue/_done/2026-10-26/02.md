Manchester City’s current squad contains zero players signed during their 2009–2018 financial breach window.

📊 An independent commission found Premier League financial rule breaches between 2009 and 2018, a period City continue to challenge on appeal. However, rapid squad turnover means no active player purchased from an outside club was bought during those nine years.

🇵🇹 The longest-tenured external signing still at the Etihad is Ruben Dias, who arrived from Benfica in September 2020. Erling Haaland follows next, having signed from Borussia Dortmund in the summer of 2022, highlighting how modern City’s core is.

🎓 Phil Foden remains the sole squad member who played during the charged years after debuting in November 2017. Because Foden developed in the club's academy, his career involved no transfer market transactions or fee structures.
