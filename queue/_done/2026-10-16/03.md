Harry Kane has agreed a deal in principle to extend his Bayern Munich stay until 2029.

📌 Reports confirm Bayern Munich and Harry Kane have reached an agreement in principle on a contract extension running through summer 2029. Paperwork is currently being finalized following negotiations driven by Kane's dominant goalscoring output.

📊 Since joining from Tottenham, Kane has racked up 154 goals in 156 appearances. He recently shattered the record for fastest player to 100 Bundesliga goals, taking just 98 games and beating a 50-year-old record by 31 matches.

🏆 Having secured two Bundesliga titles and individual Golden Shoes while maintaining elite physical condition in his thirties, Bayern are committing long-term to ensure their attack remains world-class.
