🏆 Graeme Souness was top of the world in 1984, but snubbed Barcelona for an ambitious Italian project.

🇮🇹 Right after captaining Liverpool to their fourth European Cup victory against Roma, midfielder Graeme Souness was one of football's premier stars, finishing sixth in that year's Ballon d'Or. Barcelona stepped in with a contract offer under new manager Terry Venables as Diego Maradona departed for Napoli.

💼 However, personal family rules dictated his next move. Souness required a three-year stint outside the UK due to his former wife's inheritance setup. When Barcelona offered only a two-year deal, Souness rejected the Catalans and signed for Sampdoria in a £650,000 transfer.

🇮🇹 At the time, Serie A was becoming the epicenter of world football under ambitious owners like Paolo Mantovani. Souness won the Coppa Italia during his two years in Genoa before moving to Rangers as player-manager in 1986.
