MLS clubs spent a record $190 million this summer, driven by overhauled roster rules and European player sales. 💸 The league's summer expenditure surged by 65% year-over-year, bringing total 2026 market spending past $410 million. Rule updates allowing direct cash transfers alongside General Allocation Money (GAM) have completely reshaped how franchises operate. 🔄 Teams can now convert up to $3 million of transfer proceeds into GAM annually to buy down salary cap hits. Meanwhile, high-value exports of young stars like Lucas Herrington ($17m) and Zavier Gozo ($15m) to European clubs are generating unprecedented revenue. 📈 What do you think of MLS's evolving transfer model?
