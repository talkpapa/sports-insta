🇺🇸 David Beckham is officially on the ballot for the US Soccer Hall of Fame, sparking debate over how foreign stars are honored in America. Nominated in the "veteran" category, the English midfielder follows a long precedent of overseas icons like Pelé and Franz Beckenbauer who shaped the US game.

📈 His impact wasn't just commercial; it was structural. To facilitate his 2007 move to LA Galaxy, MLS established the Designated Player rule, fundamentally changing squad building. He won two MLS Cups and two Supporters' Shields during his playing career, proving he wasn't just a marketing gimmick.

🏢 Crucially, Beckham's contract included a discounted franchise expansion option. This clause paved the way for him to found Inter Miami, cementing a lasting infrastructure that later attracted Lionel Messi, ensuring his American legacy survived long after he stopped playing.
