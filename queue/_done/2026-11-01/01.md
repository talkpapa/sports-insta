🎯 Roma secured 19-year-old playmaker Mora for just €25m despite a previous €70m valuation. Here is how the deal played out.

📉 Mora was on the radar of Europe's top clubs, with Porto expecting his full €70m (£59.5m) buy-out clause to be activated. However, after falling out of favour under new head coach Francesco Farioli, his valuation took a sharp hit.

💡 Roma acted quickly to negotiate a €25m (£21.4m) base fee. To convince Porto, Roma included a 50% sell-on clause that contains a future buyout option. The player pushed for the move specifically to play under Gian Piero Gasperini, already scoring against Lecce.
