Dominic Calvert-Lewin has committed his long-term future to Leeds United with a new contract running until 2029.

📝 Leeds United have officially secured Dominic Calvert-Lewin on a new contract through to June 2029. The 29-year-old striker joined in August 2025 and established himself immediately, netting 15 goals across all competitions in his debut campaign at Elland Road.

📈 His impressive form has continued into the current season, registering five goals and an assist with Leeds sitting seventh in the Premier League table. That output earned him an England recall under Thomas Tuchel, alongside recent reports linking him with a potential move to Manchester United.

🔒 By securing his extension now, Leeds protect a vital core asset, head off external transfer interest, and ensure stability at the top of their attack for years to come.
