Arsenal signed big names early on but failed to secure a marquee attacker, leaving Mikel Arteta's frontline short on depth. 🔴 Arsenal's transfer window started strongly with Christos Tzolis, Bruno Guimarães, and Ezri Konsa arriving. However, their search for a star attacker ended in frustration after missing out on key targets. ❌ The Gunners targeted Morgan Rogers before pivoting to Vinícius Júnior, who eventually signed a new deal with Real Madrid. Late interest in Julián Alvarez was also rejected by the player, forcing Arsenal to keep their powder dry. 🇸🇦 Despite these failures, the club still sanctioned the exits of Gabriel Jesus to Barcelona and Ethan Nwaneri to Dortmund. With Gabriel Martinelli expected to join Al-Hilal, any injuries could leave Arteta with a major crisis. Will Arsenal regret not panic-buying a striker?
