Jamie Vardy is back in English football, but his task at Burnley is a massive rescue mission. Burnley have had a disastrous start to their Championship campaign. Relegated last season, they are currently bottom of the table and winless after five games under manager Nicky Hayen, prompting this sudden move for the veteran striker. Vardy, who turns 40 in January, was a free agent after a stint in Italy with Cremonese. Although a romantic return to struggling Leicester City in League One was heavily rumored, the striker completely ruled out that reunion before choosing Turf Moor. The short-term contract runs until the end of the season. Despite a booming media and podcasting career, Vardy is determined to keep playing and help steer the struggling Clarets away from successive relegations. Can Vardy save Burnley from relegation to League One?
