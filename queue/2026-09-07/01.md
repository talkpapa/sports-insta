Arsenal chose to sign no one rather than panic-buy a striker. Here is the strategy behind their quiet window.

⚽ Arsenal's summer transfer window ended without the blockbuster forward many fans expected. High-profile targets like Julian Alvarez eluded the club, but instead of chasing compromised alternatives, Mikel Arteta chose to trust his existing squad. The key is keeping their premier attacking trio healthy.

📈 When Martin Odegaard, Kai Havertz, and Bukayo Saka are on the pitch together, the results speak for themselves. The last time they stayed fit for a full campaign in 2023/24, Arsenal racked up a club-record 91 Premier League goals. Already this term, both Odegaard and Havertz have netted three goals in four games.

🤝 The mutual understanding between the trio, particularly Odegaard and Havertz, provides a fluid movement that static signings cannot easily replicate. While a marquee striker remains the ideal long-term dream, Arsenal are betting that chemistry and physical fitness will bridge the gap.

Can Arsenal win the league without signing a clinical number nine?
