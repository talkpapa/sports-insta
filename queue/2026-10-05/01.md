⚽️ Arsenal have broken their transfer sale record by sending Gabriel Martinelli to Al-Hilal in a £60m package.

🇸🇦 Al-Hilal have secured the 25-year-old Brazilian on a reported four-year contract worth £17m per season. Arsenal receive a club-record £60m fee—surpassing the £40m they got for Alex Oxlade-Chamberlain in 2017—and have negotiated a crucial sell-on clause for any future move.

🔴 Despite Arsenal winning the Premier League last season, Martinelli's influence had waned as he fell down the pecking order under Mikel Arteta. With Gabriel Jesus also departing for Barcelona, the Gunners are clearing out squad depth without making immediate deadline-day replacements.
