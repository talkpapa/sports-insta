🇮🇪 Tottenham broke a League of Ireland record to secure 18-year-old forward Melia, but immediately loaned him to Lincoln.

What makes Melia unique is his senior experience. While most 18-year-olds are playing youth academy matches, Melia has already racked up nearly 100 senior appearances for St Patrick’s Athletic. He is a natural finisher who can lead the line or drop deep to link play, giving him a massive physical edge.

The move to Lincoln is highly strategic. Lincoln have struggled for cutting edge up front, recently managing just a single shot on goal from 18 attempts. Melia gets guaranteed first-team minutes in a physical league, while Lincoln get the clinical finisher they desperately need.

Will Melia prove to be the bargain of the season for Lincoln?
