Bradley Barcola dreamed of Liverpool as a teenager, and now his £123m move to Anfield is official. 🇫🇷 The 22-year-old French winger leaves Paris Saint-Germain after three seasons, joining Liverpool in a massive transaction that represents a record departure for the French champions. Having originally joined PSG from Lyon for €45m, his growth on the international stage boosted his transfer value significantly. ⚽ The turning point in Paris came after the arrival of Khvicha Kvaratskhelia, which restricted Barcola's opportunities on the left wing during key Champions League knockout matches. With PSG seeking squad balance and huge financial return, selling him fit into a summer where they raised record transfer revenue. 🔴 For Liverpool, adding a dynamic forward who explicitly wanted the move provides freshness and width to their attack. Barcola's tactical fit on the left flank gives Anfield a direct threat who enters his peak years with top-level European experience.
