Pedro Neto chose Stamford Bridge over Manchester City, and Chelsea rewarded him immediately.

 Portuguese international Pedro Neto had strong summer interest from Manchester City, but opted to stay and play under Xabi Alonso. Chelsea responded by handing the 26-year-old winger an improved five-year contract with the option of an additional year, including a deserved pay raise.

 While midfielder Enzo Fernández forced a £125m exit to City after pushing for a move, Neto showed full commitment to the project. Chelsea’s owners are sending a clear signal across the squad: players who commit and perform will be rewarded financially, regardless of how many years remain on their existing deals.
