Roma just pulled off one of the best value transfers of the entire summer window. ⚡

📌 Porto originally anticipated that Mora’s full €70m (£59.5m) release clause would be triggered by a European powerhouse in 2025. However, after failing to land a bidder at that price point, the 19-year-old playmaker fell out of favor under new head coach Francesco Farioli, drastically reducing Porto's leverage.

📌 Roma capitalized on the situation by negotiating a baseline fee of just €25m (£21.4m). To bridge the gap in valuation, Porto insisted on a heavy 50% sell-on clause, though Roma secured an option to buy out that percentage at a later date.

📌 The lure of playing under Gian Piero Gasperini was pivotal in convincing Mora to choose Serie A, where he has already opened his goalscoring account. Was this the best piece of business done this summer?
