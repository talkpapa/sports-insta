Roma landed 19-year-old playmaker Mora for €25m, a fraction of his €70m valuation. Here is how they pulled it off.

📉 Just a year ago, Europe's elite expected Mora's €70m release clause to be activated. But after falling out of favour under new Porto head coach Francesco Farioli, his situation changed drastically, opening a brief transfer window.

💡 Roma moved quickly, pitching a structure Porto couldn't ignore: €25m upfront combined with a 50% sell-on clause that Roma retain the option to buy out later. This protected Porto's future value while lowering Roma's immediate outlay.

🇮🇹 The move was also driven by Mora's desire to work under Gian Piero Gasperini, fitting perfectly into the manager's tactical system. With a goal against Lecce already under his belt, the bargain deal is paying off immediately.
