Bruno Fernandes has rejected a second contract extension from Manchester United, but Old Trafford officials still hold the high ground.

📌 The 32-year-old playmaker enters the final months of his current deal after turning down proposals in both the summer and recent weeks. However, United retain a club-held one-year extension option, effectively blocking him from agreeing to a pre-contract with overseas suitors like Al Hilal come January.

🧠 The dynamic shifts further with Michael Carrick's departure and the imminent arrival of Mauricio Pochettino. Fernandes nearly joined Pochettino at Tottenham back in 2019, creating an immediate tactical alignment with the incoming head coach.

⚽ Fresh off a Premier League record 21-assist campaign, the Portuguese international remains vital to United's setup despite stalled negotiations.
