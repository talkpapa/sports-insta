⚽️ A difficult period at Tyneside is finally approaching a resolution for staff and players. After missing September wages, the club confirms that the financial lifeline from new investors is currently held up by cross-border banking delays.

Following an agreement in principle with an American investment firm, an emergency loan was structured to settle immediate debts. While the capital was released last week, the physical movement of funds between international banks takes several working days to process, pushing the expected arrival date to this Tuesday.

This investment is a critical step as the club seeks stability while sitting at the bottom of the table. With a takeover process underway and an exclusivity agreement now signed, the focus turns toward finding a new manager to steady their campaign after a challenging start to the season.
