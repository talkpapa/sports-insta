🚨 Crystal Palace Women have been deducted three points following a substitution error on the opening day of the WSL season.

⚠️ The Eagles defeated Everton 1-0, but manager Jo Potter brought on a late substitute in the 97th minute. While teams are allowed five substitutions per match, they must be completed across a maximum of three stoppages. This late change marked Palace's fourth separate stoppage window.

📊 An independent tribunal determined that responsibility for following the rule lies solely with the club, not match officials. Even though the player was on the pitch for just 46 seconds, the panel issued a points deduction to enforce rules and deter future violations.

📉 The decision drops Palace to 13th place into the relegation play-off spot on goal difference. Palace released a statement expressing disappointment, calling the sanction unreasonable and disproportionate.
