Premier League clubs signed 100 teenagers in five years, spending massive fees to control the global talent pipeline early. 💰 By spending lavish fees like £81.5m on teenage talent before they reach their prime, Premier League sides are locking down top prospects far earlier than European rivals. Over the past five seasons, English top-flight teams secured 100 permanent teenage transfers, compared to just 29 in La Liga and 47 in Ligue 1. 🔄 This early recruitment drive feeds directly into the domestic transfer market. Squad cost ratio regulations encourage high player turnover, forcing English clubs to trade with each other because the league already holds most of the continent's premier young talent. 📉 As a result, European giants can no longer hang onto teenage prodigies when English offers arrive. Will rival European leagues ever be able to compete with England's financial grip on young talent?
