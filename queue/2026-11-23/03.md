Tottenham face a late squad puzzle that could push Richarlison to Trabzonspor before Friday's Turkish deadline.

⚽ While the Premier League transfer window closed on Tuesday, clubs in Turkey can still register new signings until Friday night. Tottenham manager Roberto De Zerbi confirmed that Brazilian forward Richarlison requested to leave multiple times this summer, having previously turned down a potential return to Everton.

📊 Beyond the player's desire for minutes, Spurs have a clear mathematical incentive to complete the transaction. Tottenham currently carry 18 senior non-homegrown players, which exceeds the Premier League registration limit by one. Offloading Richarlison would solve their squad balance without leaving them short.

📝 Reports indicate that personal terms between Trabzonspor and the 29-year-old forward are already in place. With one year remaining on his contract, a move to Turkey offers both parties a quick resolution before the deadline shuts.

Will Richarlison revive his career in Turkey, or should Spurs keep him for depth?
