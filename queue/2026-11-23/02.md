🚨 Folarin Balogun’s proposed £40m move from Monaco to Everton collapsed in dramatic fashion on deadline day, leaving two major transfers dead in the water. 📊 According to Monaco chief executive Thiago Scuro, Everton raised unfounded doubts during Balogun’s medical in Liverpool, causing reports to quickly leak that the striker had failed his tests. When Everton resolved their concerns hours later and signed the deal sheet alongside Monaco, Balogun refused to sign his part, citing a total loss of trust over how Everton’s medical department handled his health status. 🔄 The late drama had a direct domino effect on Chelsea. Monaco had agreed a £47m deal to send Lamine Camara to Stamford Bridge as financial coverage, but once Everton initially agreed to sign Balogun, Monaco pulled the plug on Camara’s move. When Balogun subsequently walked away, both deals were left completely dead. Did Balogun make the right choice by walking away from Everton?
