🇸🇦 Arsenal have secured a club-record £60m fee for Gabriel Martinelli as he joins Saudi champions Al-Hilal.

The Brazilian winger has signed a lucrative four-year contract in the Middle East, reportedly worth around £17m per season. After falling down the pecking order at the Emirates, the 25-year-old decided to make the move before the Saudi transfer window closes this Sunday.

For Arsenal, the deal represents pure profit on a player signed from Ituano in 2019, shattering their previous record sale of £40m. The Gunners also secured a sell-on clause in the agreement, though they opted against signing a direct replacement on deadline day.
