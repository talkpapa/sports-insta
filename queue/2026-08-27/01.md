🚨 Liverpool have made a huge £120m breakthrough for Bradley Barcola, but the blockbuster deal raises massive tactical questions.

While the PSG forward represents a world-class addition, critics like Jamie Carragher point out that Barcola primarily operates on the left. Liverpool's most urgent vacancy is on the right wing following Mohamed Salah's departure, a gap the club is still scrambling to fill.

Financially, this deal takes Liverpool's summer spending past £663m, following the high-profile arrival of Florian Wirtz. To balance the squad and the books, Barcola's arrival is expected to immediately greenlight Cody Gakpo's departure, with interest from Manchester City and Tottenham.

Meanwhile, the search for a true right-winger continues. Talks with Brighton for Yankuba Minteh have stalled over an £80m valuation, and Crystal Palace are currently refusing to sell alternative target Ismaila Sarr.
