Nottingham Forest have broken their transfer record to sign Liam Delap for £50m, despite his tough spell at Chelsea.

🚨 Nottingham Forest have completed a club-record £50m deal for Chelsea striker Liam Delap, who signs a five-year contract. Despite scoring just one league goal last season, Chelsea secured a £20m profit on the young forward after a chaotic year at Stamford Bridge plagued by injuries and three managerial changes.

📈 The massive fee reflects his immense potential shown at Ipswich, where he scored 12 goals in a struggling side. Delap’s game is built on power and directness—he led Premier League strikers with 26 shot-ending carries in 2024-25. At Chelsea, slow build-ups and possession play completely neutralised his main weapon.

🔄 Now, manager Oliver Glasner aims to rebuild Delap's confidence by playing to his transition strengths. If Forest can recreate the direct, counter-attacking space he enjoyed before his Chelsea move, they might have secured the country's most promising young direct runner.
