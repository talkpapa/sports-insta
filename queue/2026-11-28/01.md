Manchester United have officially taken action to protect Bruno Fernandes' market value before the January window opens.

🔴 By triggering the one-year option in his deal, United have bound their captain through to 2028. This move effectively blocks foreign clubs from initiating pre-contract negotiations when the winter transfer window opens on January 1.

💼 Long-term renewal discussions have been paused until the club's league standings improve, according to sources. Although United were open to listening to offers from Saudi Pro League side Al Hilal in summer 2025, keeping control of his contract now ensures they command a significant fee should an exit materialize.

📊 Fernandes has registered over 100 goals for the club since arriving from Sporting in 2020. Securing his status gives the board critical leverage while the squad focuses on regaining form under Mauricio Pochettino.
