🚨 Premier League rivals are privately pushing the Independent Football Regulator to force a full sale of Manchester City.

🏛️ Following ruling details of £900m in inflated commercial arrangements between 2009 and 2018, several top-flight executives anonymously told BBC Sport that point deductions or fines would be meaningless. They argue that allowing the existing hierarchy to retain control damages the entire league's credibility.

⚖️ The newly formed Independent Football Regulator possesses statutory powers to deem directors unfit and mandate compulsory club sales in severe circumstances. Club officials question why they should pay regulatory levies if watchdogs hesitate to exercise their ultimate authority on high-profile breaches.

🌍 While political and trade implications complicate potential divestment decisions, the watchdog insists statutory duties will take priority over financial stature once appeals conclude.
