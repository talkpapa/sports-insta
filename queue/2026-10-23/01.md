Liverpool refused to spend crazy money on short-term fixes despite missing key players through injury.

🧠 Head coach Andoni Iraola explained that Liverpool decided against overpaying for mid-tier positional cover while players like Hugo Ekitiké, Conor Bradley, and Giovanni Leoni recover from serious injuries. The board prioritized long-term squad vision over quick band-aids.

📊 Despite looking into targets like Yankuba Minteh and Ismaïla Sarr, Liverpool opted to trust versatile wingers like Bradley Barcola, Cody Gakpo, and Rio Ngumoha across both flanks. Iraola emphasized that adaptable quality outweighs single-position stopgaps in the current market.

💰 With heavy expenditure already committed to primary targets like Alexander Isak, Florian Wirtz, and Barcola, saving budget for future priorities made more sense than overpaying for short-term fixes.
