⚽ Roma completed one of the sharpest deals of the window by landing Porto's 19-year-old playmaker Mora for just €25m. Porto initially anticipated clubs triggering his €70m release clause in 2025, but a shift in coaching left the youngster out of favour and opened the door for a swift movement. 💼 To bridge the gap between Porto's valuation and the reduced fee, Roma included a 50% sell-on clause with a buy-out option. The tactical vision of Gian Piero Gasperini was central to convincing the player, who felt the style suited his creative profile. 📊 Having already netted his first goal in Serie A, this structured transfer allows Roma to secure top talent within financial constraints.
