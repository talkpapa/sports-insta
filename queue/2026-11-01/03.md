Promoted Premier League clubs just shattered transfer records, spending almost £200M each in one summer.

💰 Back in 2021, the three promoted teams combined spent just £98.8m total. Fast forward to this window, and Ipswich Town alone splashed £194.7m on 15 new arrivals to completely remake their squad.

📈 Premier League financial dominance has widened the quality gap dramatically. Teams like Hull City added 18 players for £156.5m and Coventry spent £122.4m, proving that sticking with Championship squads is no longer a realistic survival strategy.

🔄 Meanwhile, leagues like Ligue 1 operate in reverse, with 15 of 18 French clubs turning a profit by selling marquee talent like Bradley Barcola for £123m to fund their operations.

Will this massive spending spree actually guarantee Premier League survival?
