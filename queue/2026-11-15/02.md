None of Manchester City's current senior signings were purchased during the 2009 to 2018 financial breach window.
⚽ The Premier League financial regulation charges focus specifically on a nine-year window between 2009 and 2018. City have denied all charges and appealed the findings, but questions remained regarding how the roster was built during that period.
📊 A breakdown of the current squad shows that the longest-serving purchased player is Ruben Dias, who arrived in 2020 from Benfica. Erling Haaland followed in 2022, meaning all bought players in the squad post-date the infraction timeframe.
🎓 Phil Foden is the sole current first-team player who featured for the club before 2018, having debuted in November 2017. However, because Foden emerged through City's academy system, no transfer fee or senior signing contract was executed during the breach years.
